How Should a Dental Office Handle Phone Calls When It Closes for the Holidays?

Direct answer

Write down every closed day, half day, and reduced-hour day from Thanksgiving through New Year's. Put those hours on Google, the website, and the phone greeting before the first change. Name who handles urgent calls under the practice's own protocol, and decide how routine calls get answered while the office is closed.

Holiday hours Last updated: November 16, 2026 8 min read By Aria Dental

This page is for a practice owner or office manager planning the phone from Thanksgiving through New Year's Day.

Which days should a dental office close or shorten from Thanksgiving through New Year's?

Mark every date closed, a half day, reduced hours, or open on the normal schedule. Do that before you change Google or the greeting.

In 2026 the stretch runs from Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, through New Year's Day, Friday, January 1, 2027. Google's holiday list for the United States includes Thanksgiving (November 26), Black Friday (November 27), Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve, and New Year's Day. On those dates, Google says a warning may appear on the Business Profile that hours might differ.

  • Thursday, November 26, Thanksgiving Day.
  • Friday, November 27: closed, a morning only, or open.
  • Thursday, December 24, Christmas Eve. If the last patient is at noon, write noon.
  • Friday, December 25, Christmas Day.
  • Monday, December 28 through Wednesday, December 30. Decide each day on its own.
  • Thursday, December 31, New Year's Eve, and Friday, January 1, New Year's Day.

A normal Saturday and Sunday stays on weekend dental call coverage. Use this page for a holiday closure, a half day, or the short days between Christmas and New Year's.

How do you put those hours on the Google Business Profile?

Use special hours so the regular weekly hours stay in place. Save them before the first day that changes.

Google's help page on special hours says you can set them when hours change for a short time, including holidays. Confirm official holiday hours even when they match a regular week.

Open the Business Profile, select Edit profile, then Hours, and next to Special hours select Edit. Leave Closed checked on a closed day. On a half day, uncheck Closed and enter the open and close times. Select Save. Special hours show only when regular hours are set to "Open with main hours." Enter each date on its own, such as noon on Christmas Eve and a closed Christmas Day.

Special hours cover a closure of up to six days in a row. Google's page on marking a business closed says to mark the profile Temporarily closed when the closure is more than seven days. A full shutdown from December 24 through January 1 is nine days, so both pages point to Temporarily closed. Google says those listings can rank after open businesses for broad queries. Switch the profile back to open when you reopen. If a day in between is open, even for short hours, keep that day on special hours. Google's holidays page says to set special hours in advance.

What should the website and the phone greeting say?

Put the same dates, stop times, and reopen morning on the website and in the greeting.

Add a short banner on the homepage and on the hours page: closed dates, half days with the stop time, and when you are back. If December 28 is an open morning, say so. Take the banner down when the normal schedule returns.

Switch the greeting the afternoon before the first closed or shortened day. Use the holiday script on the voicemail greeting page, and fill in the holiday, the reopen day, and the hours. For a half day, say you are open until the published stop time. Call the line and listen through to the beep. If the number forwards, match it to those hours, including a half-day stop. Who should handle urgent calls during the closure?

Urgent calls follow the protocol the practice already uses. Write who is responsible on these dates, how a caller is directed, and what the main line may say.

Give that note to the greeting, the answering service, the forwarded phone, and Aria. If the protocol is a message the doctor reviews the next open morning, the greeting says that. If it names a person for a specific afternoon, that person has the dates and a way to be reached that the practice controls.

What should the first morning back look like?

Treat the first staffed morning as its own block. The list holds every closed day and every afternoon a half day pushed forward. Visits that fit a real opening should already be on the schedule, and anything that needs a person should be a note with who called, why, and what they were told.

Clearing live rings while that list waits is the same kind of morning as the Monday morning backlog, including a reopen on the Friday after Thanksgiving or on Monday, January 4. One person owns the leftover list, and someone stays with the live lines. Before the first patient, Google hours match today, the banner matches today, and the greeting is the open-office greeting.

Frequently asked questions

When should a dental office put holiday hours on Google?

Set special hours before the first changed day. Google's holidays page says a warning may appear that hours might differ, and it says to set special hours in advance. Google also says it is a good idea to confirm official holiday hours even when they match a regular week.

What if the office is closed from Christmas Eve through New Year's Day?

Count the closed days in a row. Google's special-hours page says special hours cover up to six closed days in a row, and says to mark Temporarily closed at seven days or more. Google's page on marking a business closed says to use special hours for seven days or less, and Temporarily closed for more than seven days. December 24 through January 1 with no day open is nine days, and both pages point to Temporarily closed.

Should a half day get its own phone greeting?

Yes. Say the office is open until the real stop time, and say when messages will be returned. Use the holiday greeting on the voicemail page, with that stop time, the reopen day, and the hours filled in.

Who handles urgent calls during a holiday closure?

The person named in the practice's own protocol for those dates. Write the path down and give it to everyone covering the phone. Urgent calls stay on that protocol.

Does Aria call patients back over the holiday?

No. Aria answers inbound calls only. Requests that need a person during the closure are logged for the team under the practice's rules.

How is a holiday closure different from a normal weekend on the phone?

Weekend coverage is for a normal Saturday and Sunday. A holiday closure adds half days, reduced hours, and gaps of several days, including the days between Christmas and New Year's, plus a longer list on the first morning back.
