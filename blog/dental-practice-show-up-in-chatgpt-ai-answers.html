How Does a Dental Practice Show Up in ChatGPT and AI Search Answers?

A practice can be cited when its website is crawlable, the public facts match the Google Business Profile, and the pages answer common questions in plain sentences. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews each have their own rules. None of them guarantees a recommendation.

AI Search Last updated: November 4, 2026 8 min read By Aria Dental

This page is for a practice owner or office manager.

What do ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews actually read?

They read public web pages they are allowed to crawl, or pages already in a search index. They do not read the practice management software, the schedule, or a note that never left the office.

Google's guide to AI features and your website says AI Overviews and AI Mode are part of Google Search. A supporting link has to be indexed and eligible to show a snippet. There are no extra technical requirements, and no special AI file or schema is required. Crawling has to be allowed in robots.txt, important facts should be text, structured data should match that text, and the Business Profile should be up to date. Meeting the list does not mean Google will crawl, index, or serve the page.

OpenAI's crawler documentation says OAI-SearchBot surfaces sites in ChatGPT search answers. Opting out keeps the site out of those answers, though it can still appear as a navigational link. OpenAI's help page on searching the web says ChatGPT sometimes partners with other search providers, rewrites the question it sends them, and can use general location for local results. Placement is not guaranteed. Allow OAI-SearchBot, and allow the host or CDN to accept OpenAI's published searchbot IP addresses.

Perplexity's crawler documentation says PerplexityBot surfaces and links sites in Perplexity search results, and that it is not used to crawl content for foundation models. Perplexity recommends allowing it in robots.txt. Perplexity-User may open a page when someone asks a question, and Perplexity says that fetch generally ignores robots.txt.

What should match on the website and the Google Business Profile?

The practice name, the hours, the services you book, and the phone number that rings that office. When those four disagree, an answer can quote the outdated one.

Walk categories, hours, photos, Q&A, posts, and reviews on the Google Business Profile checklist. This page does not repeat that list. The phone-number post decides which number is public. Put that same number on the website.

Reviews are public text on that same profile. Keep it current so they describe the same practice as the site. Neither Google nor OpenAI says a star rating places a practice in an answer.

What should each service page say in plain sentences?

Give each service patients search for and book its own page, and write it so one paragraph answers the question without the rest of the site.

The separate-page post is the build guide: which services get a URL, what belongs on the page, and why a thin copy or a city page is a different problem. State the service, who it is for, what the visit is, and how to book. A short FAQ on that page is a reply a tool can quote. Google says important content should be text, and that AI Overviews and AI Mode may run several related searches while building an answer.

Which crawlers should a dental site leave unblocked?

Leave Googlebot, OAI-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot allowed if you want those products to be able to cite the site.

  1. Googlebot. Google says robots.txt rules for Googlebot control Search, including AI Overviews and AI Mode. Google-Extended is separate: it limits training and grounding in some of Google's other systems. Blocking it leaves AI Overviews on the Googlebot rule. nosnippet, data-nosnippet, max-snippet, and noindex limit what Search can show.
  2. OAI-SearchBot. This is the ChatGPT search crawler. GPTBot is separate and may be used for training, so a site can allow OAI-SearchBot and disallow GPTBot. ChatGPT-User may open a page a person asks about. OpenAI says robots.txt may not apply to that visit, and that it does not decide search inclusion. A robots.txt change can take about 24 hours.
  3. PerplexityBot. Allow it if Perplexity search should be able to link the site. A robots.txt change can take up to 24 hours. Perplexity-User fetches a page because a person asked, and that fetch generally ignores robots.txt.
  4. The host, not only the file. A firewall or CDN that challenges unrecognized bots will stop a crawler robots.txt allows. OpenAI says the host has to allow its published searchbot IP addresses. Perplexity publishes PerplexityBot ranges on the crawler page. Those ranges change, so read the current page before pasting them into a firewall.

Does Dentist or LocalBusiness schema get the practice named?

No. Markup can describe the practice to Google, and it does not reserve a sentence in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or an AI Overview.

Google's AI features guide says those features need no special schema, and that structured data has to match the visible text. Google's Local Business documentation covers rich results: a name and a physical address are required, using the most specific LocalBusiness subtype you can. schema.org defines Dentist as a type with LocalBusiness properties, including opening hours. Repeat the name, address, and hours already visible on the page. Google does not guarantee the rich result will show.

Why does a citation still depend on the phone being answered?

A citation sends a person to a page or a phone number. The visit exists when that call is answered.

Allow OAI-SearchBot to crawl the site, and make sure the host or CDN allows OpenAI's published searchbot IP addresses. What does a page need to be eligible for a Google AI Overview?

Google says the page must be indexed and eligible to show in Search with a snippet. AI Overviews and AI Mode have no extra technical requirements and no special schema. Allow Googlebot, keep the important facts in text, and keep the Business Profile up to date. Meeting that list does not mean Google will crawl, index, or show the page.

Can a dental office block GPTBot and still be eligible for ChatGPT search?

Yes. OAI-SearchBot is the crawler for ChatGPT search answers. GPTBot is separate and crawls content that may train foundation models. Allow OAI-SearchBot and disallow GPTBot if you want search eligibility without that training crawl. Disallowing OAI-SearchBot keeps a site out of ChatGPT search answers. OpenAI says a robots.txt change can take about 24 hours to affect search results.
