How Should a Dental Office Handle Cancellation and Reschedule Calls?

Direct answer

When a patient calls to cancel or move a visit, do not only delete it. Confirm which appointment it is, offer the next opening that fits that visit on the same call, and write a short non-clinical reason in the schedule note. Release the old chair so the next inbound caller can take it.

Scheduling Last updated Nov 9, 2026 7 min read By Aria Dental

This page is for a practice owner or office manager who wants one way to handle a patient calling to cancel or move a visit. Use the same steps when the desk is with a patient, at lunch, or already gone. A patient who never calls is covered in how to reduce dental no-shows.

What should the front desk say when a patient calls to cancel or move a visit?

Confirm which visit is on the book, then offer the next opening that fits it before you delete it. Deleting the visit and saying "call us later" leaves an empty chair.

  1. Confirm the caller and the visit: name, day, time, and visit type. If someone else is calling, write both names.
  2. Ask whether they want to move it or only cancel.
  3. Offer the next opening for that visit type. Give two times when both are open.
  4. If they take one, move the visit and read it back: "You are now on [day] at [time]. The earlier time is off the book."
  5. If they decline, cancel, release the chair, and note that a time was offered.
  6. Write a short reason in their words. Stop before symptoms, medications, or treatment detail.
  7. If the visit is today, or the room is already set, tell the provider or assistant whose chair it was.

What should you capture on the call, and what should you leave out?

Capture who called, which visit, the outcome, and a short non-clinical reason. Leave clinical detail out of the scheduling note.

"Work conflict," "no ride," "out of town," or "not feeling well" is enough. Pain, swelling, a medication, or a post-procedure question goes to the practice. Do not paste symptoms into the appointment note. Still finish the schedule change on this call. Spell the name back if the chart is not obvious. If someone else is calling, write both names.

What should you say, and what should the note contain, for each kind of call?

Match the line to the call. The note should show the outcome without a replay of the conversation.

Call What to say What to log
Wants to move "You are on [day] at [time] for [visit]. I have [time] and [time]. Which should I keep?" Old time, new time, short reason, old chair released.
Might rebook "Before I take this off, the next opening is [time]. Should I move you there?" Time offered, accepted or declined. Release the chair if they decline.
Declines every time "I will take [day] at [time] off the book. Call when you want a new time." Declined, reason in their words, slot released.
Fee decision "That decision goes to the office. I will have them call you back. I can still move or cancel the visit now." The ask, who it was sent to, and whether the visit changed. No dollar amount.
Clinical question "I can still move the visit. The office will follow up on that question." Scheduling outcome only. No symptoms in the note.

The FAQ says Aria does not decide a fee waiver. It sends a callback with who called and why. Do the same if you cannot decide. This page does not set a fee.

What belongs in the practice-management note?

Write it while the caller is still on the line: original visit, outcome, new time if there is one, short reason, and whether today's provider was told.

Change the appointment itself. A comment under a visit that still holds the chair does not free it. Open Dental write-back is live. Google Calendar sync is available. Dentrix and Eaglesoft write-back are on the roadmap, not a live native connection. Where write-back is not live, the front desk changes that schedule before hanging up. Name who handled the call, and keep clinical detail out of the note.

What do you do with the chair that just opened?

Release it on the live schedule during the same call, so the next inbound caller who wants that visit type can take it.

Take the old time off before you hang up, whether you moved them or they declined. Offer that opening to the next person who calls for that visit type. If the office already keeps its own list of patients who asked to come sooner, the staff works that list. Aria does not call or text other patients to fill the chair, and it does not place outbound recall.

Who needs to know about the change?

The schedule is the notice. Tell a person when the change hits today's chair, a room already set, or a decision the desk cannot make.

  • The provider or assistant for that chair, when the visit is today or the room is already set.
  • The office manager, through the note, so the board matches what the caller agreed to.
  • The person named for a fee decision or a clinical question: who called, which visit, and what the caller was told.

How should these calls be handled when the desk is busy, at lunch, or closed?

Keep the same script. The only change is who answers.

If the person at the window cannot offer a new time, another path has to confirm the visit, offer an opening, and write the result.

At lunch, a cancel still needs a new time or a clean release, not a greeting that says to call back later. Lunch-hour coverage is who answers then.

After close, the caller is often moving tomorrow's visit. Frequently asked questions

What should the front desk say when a patient calls to cancel?

Confirm the visit and offer the next opening before you delete it. If they accept, move them and read the new time back. If they decline, release the chair and note that a time was offered.

What should the scheduling note include?

The original visit, whether it moved or was cancelled, any new time, a short reason in the caller's words, and whether today's provider was told. Leave out symptoms, medications, and diagnoses.

Does Aria call other patients to fill a cancellation?

No. Aria answers inbound calls. It can move or cancel a visit when that patient calls. It does not place outbound calls to fill the open chair, and it does not run outbound recall.

Can a cancel or reschedule be handled when the desk is at lunch or closed?

Yes, on the inbound lines the practice assigns, including after hours. The reschedule demo updates the calendar when a returning patient moves a visit. A fee-waiver decision goes back to the practice as a callback.

Can Aria write a reschedule into Dentrix or Eaglesoft?

Open Dental write-back is live, and Google Calendar sync is available. Dentrix and Eaglesoft write-back are on the roadmap, not a live native connection.

What if the caller starts describing a clinical problem?

Finish the scheduling change on this call. Clinical questions go to the practice. Do not put symptoms or a diagnosis in the scheduling note.
