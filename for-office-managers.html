The day

Four moments the line gets away from the desk.

You already know these. They are the reason the morning huddle looks calm and the voicemail box does not.

At the window

A patient is checking in. The other line rings. Your team can talk to the person in front of them or pick up the phone. The caller who waits hears voicemail.

Lunch

The desk thins out. New patients do not pause because the office is eating. Those calls still need an answer. Lunch-hour coverage.

After you lock the door

The building is closed. People still call about a first visit or a toothache. If the line goes to voicemail, you find it in the morning. After-hours answering.

Insurance on hold

Eligibility often sits on a payer line while the schedule is already moving. Aria is built to run that check during the booking call, not after the patient hangs up. Insurance on the call.

Questions office managers ask

Short answers, from the product as it is described today.

Will Aria replace the front desk?

No. Aria works alongside your team, handling overflow and after-hours calls, including the ones that ring while someone is being checked in. The people at the window stay with the patient in front of them.

Can Aria check insurance while the caller is still on the line?

Yes. During the call, Aria can collect member ID and date of birth, run eligibility through dental clearinghouses, read back deductibles and remaining benefits, estimate patient cost, and offer to collect. That includes when a parent calls for a dependent.

Does the booked visit land in Open Dental?

Open Dental write-back is live, so Aria can write the appointment back when that connection is in place. Google Calendar sync is available today. Dentrix and Eaglesoft are on the roadmap.

Can Aria take a payment on the call?

Yes. If the patient has a card on file, Aria can charge it during the call. For a new patient, Aria sends a secure payment link by text during the same conversation.

Who decides when a call goes to a person?

You do. Your team steps in on the transfer rules you set. Clinical questions — pain, medication, post-op — escalate to your team with the conversation attached. When the desk is open, Aria can forward the call. When it is not, Aria can leave a callback with the question.

How do I book a demo?

Use the demo form on the contact page. It is a 30-minute walkthrough, and the team responds within 24 hours. You can also email AriaDental@Velzyx.ai.
