The Problem: Manual Verification Costs You Hours and Patients

A patient calls. They have a toothache, they have insurance, and they want to come in tomorrow.

Then they ask the question that kills more bookings than any other:

"How much will this cost me?"

— The question every dental practice dreads. And the one most practices can't answer fast enough.

Your front desk puts them on hold. Opens the insurance portal. Enters the member ID. Waits for the system to respond. Maybe it times out. Maybe the plan details are unclear. Maybe they need to call the payer directly.

By the time they have an answer — if they get one at all — the patient has already:

Hung up. Called another practice. Decided it's too expensive. Or just never picked up when you called back.

Manual insurance verification isn't just slow. It's a revenue leak — and it happens on every single call where a patient asks about cost.

Frequently Asked Questions
Insurance verification questions.
"How does AI dental insurance verification work?"
When a patient provides their insurance information during a call or chat, Aria runs a real-time 270/271 eligibility check against 3,400+ payers. Deductibles, remaining maximums, copays, and coverage percentages are returned in seconds — while the patient is still on the line.
"How long does verification take?"
Typically under 15 seconds. The patient stays on the line while Aria checks their coverage. Aria fills the verification time with natural conversation — confirming appointment preferences or explaining what to expect — so the patient never experiences dead air.
"Which payers does Aria verify?"
Aria verifies across 3,400+ dental insurance payers — including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, United Healthcare, Humana, Principal, and virtually every major dental insurer in the United States.
"Can Aria verify insurance for dependents?"
Yes. When a parent calls for their child, Aria collects subscriber and dependent information, verifies the child's coverage under the parent's plan, and reads back benefits specific to the dependent — including pediatric-specific coverage details.
"Does this replace our current verification process?"
It eliminates the manual pre-call step. Instead of your team spending 5–10 minutes per patient in a portal, Aria handles it during the live conversation. Your team focuses on in-office patients instead of portal lookups.
"Is patient insurance data secure?"
Yes. All insurance data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest and TLS in transit. Aria is fully HIPAA compliant and operates under signed Business Associate Agreements. Member IDs and personal health information are handled per healthcare data security standards. Read more about security →
