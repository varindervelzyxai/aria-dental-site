Specialty workflow

Where emergency dental clinics practices break standard AI front office.

Generic AI receptionists treat every dental call like a cleaning request. The specialty workflows below are where they fall apart — and what we built Aria to handle for emergency dental clinics practices.

•

Calls come at 2am

Dental emergencies don't wait for business hours. The patient with the swollen face, knocked-out tooth, or post-op bleed calls when it happens. Aria answers in seconds, every time.

•

Triage requires structure

Not every after-hours call is a true emergency. Aria runs a structured triage — symptom severity, time of onset, presence of systemic signs (fever, swelling progression) — and routes accordingly: book urgent visit, escalate to on-call clinician, or schedule next-business-day if the symptom isn't urgent.

•

Pain-empathy first

Patients in pain don't want a script. They want to be heard. Aria's emergency-clinic templates lead with empathy ('that sounds really painful') before going into triage — the same approach a good front-desk team would take.

•

Walk-in slot management

Emergency clinics often hold same-day urgent slots. Aria knows which slots are emergency-only, which are flexible, and how to slot a true emergency without disrupting scheduled patients.

•

Insurance verification under time pressure

Emergency patients often haven't verified coverage. Aria runs eligibility in real-time during the call when possible, or collects what's needed for verification at check-in.

Scheduling

Specialty-aware scheduling.

Emergency clinic scheduling is fundamentally different from a regular practice. Aria's emergency-mode logic respects that.

  • → Same-day urgent slots: held until cutoff time, then released.
  • → On-call clinician routing: when symptoms exceed front-office triage scope, Aria connects to the on-call doctor.
  • → After-hours escalation policy: each clinic configures its own escalation tree.
  • → Walk-in plus appointment: Aria books the appointment but flags the patient as walk-in-likely with a note for the team.

Emergency Dental Clinics FAQ

Frequently asked questions for emergency dental clinics practices.

Does Aria triage like a clinical decision-support tool?

No. Aria runs a structured triage based on the symptom-severity protocol your clinic configures — it doesn't make clinical decisions. When a call exceeds Aria's triage scope (severe systemic signs, signs of infection spread, airway concern), it escalates to the on-call clinician or hospital ED.

How does Aria handle 2am emergency calls?

Same as a 2pm call — answer in seconds, run triage, route to the appropriate path. Most emergency clinics use Aria specifically because they don't want to staff a human at 2am for what may or may not be a true emergency.

Can Aria book true walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins get a 'walk-in-likely' flag in the appointment so the front desk knows to expect the patient. If the patient says 'I'm 10 minutes away,' Aria can confirm and let the team know.

Does Aria support multi-clinic emergency networks?

Yes — for groups operating multiple emergency clinics, Aria routes by patient location to the nearest clinic with an open emergency slot. Configured per group.
