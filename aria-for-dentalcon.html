Frequently asked questions

We're a 25-office DSO on Denticon. Can Aria run for all of us at once?
Yes — but most large groups prefer a phased rollout. We typically start with 2–3 pilot offices for the first 30 days, validate the routing rules and call patterns, then onboard the rest in waves. The infrastructure is the same regardless of group size; the rollout cadence is what changes.
Can Aria handle our existing Denticon-native call routing rules?
Aria's routing layer sits in front of any per-office call flows you already have. We can match your existing escalation patterns (transfer to billing, transfer to office manager, route emergencies to on-call) on a per-office or group-wide basis.
Does Aria conflict with Planet DDS's Patient Engagement tools?
No. If you already use Planet DDS's reminder or two-way text engine, Aria's reminders can be turned off so you don't double-message patients. Voice and inbound chat work the same regardless.
What does Aria cost at DSO scale?
Pricing scales with locations and call volume, not per-seat. Talk to us about your office count and rough monthly call volume and we'll quote a group rate. Most multi-location DSOs see lower per-office Aria spend than single-location practices because the platform overhead is shared.
Is Aria HIPAA-compliant for DSO use?
Yes. We sign a BAA with the operating entity (DSO or MSO) and apply per-location data scoping. PHI is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and in transit with TLS. See /security for the full posture.
