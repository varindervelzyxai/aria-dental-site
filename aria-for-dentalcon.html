Aria + Denticon

Aria for Denticon. The AI front office that scales with your DSO.

Built for Denticon's multi-location, cloud-first DSO model. One AI receptionist across every office. Voice + chat live today; deep PMS sync rolling through 2026.

Book a Demo →See How It Works

Denticon, by Planet DDS, is the cloud PMS most large dental groups land on when they outgrow on-prem Dentrix or Eaglesoft. It was built for multi-location from day one, which is exactly the operational shape Aria was built to support: one phone tree, one inbound chat queue, many offices, one consistent patient experience. The two systems are a natural fit. Where Aria for Open Dental is the deepest single-practice integration we ship, Aria for Denticon is the integration we built for groups — multi-location routing, location-aware booking, and DSO-scale reporting layered on top.

Status

How Aria works with Denticon

Denticon integration is in active development against Planet DDS's cloud API. Voice and chat capabilities are live today and work independently of PMS depth. Read sync (patient lookup, schedule visibility) targets Q3 2026; two-way booking and write-back targets Q4 2026.

🏢

Multi-location aware from day one

Denticon's core unit is the office — yours might be 3, 30, or 300 of them. Aria routes inbound calls to the right office based on caller history, DID, or voice prompt, and books patients into the location that's actually convenient for them.

☁️

Cloud-native, no shim

Aria authenticates to the Denticon API in the cloud. There is no on-prem agent, no Sikka middleware, no Windows service running on a server in any office. The integration is as available as Denticon itself.

👥

Cross-location patient lookup

Patients in DSOs often visit multiple offices. Aria looks up the caller across your entire Denticon tenant — not just the office they happened to dial — and surfaces the most recent visit, primary provider, and active insurance regardless of which office created the chart.

🛡️

Insurance verification

Stedi 270/271 eligibility runs in under 60 seconds and is delivered to the inbound caller live. Q4 2026 brings native write-back to the patient's Denticon insurance record.

📊

Group-level reporting

Per-office and roll-up reporting on call volume, booking conversion, and after-hours capture. Built for the DSO operations team, not just the front desk.

🔐

Scoped, revocable access

Aria uses scoped API credentials issued from your Denticon admin. Revocable any time. No shared passwords, no front-desk service accounts.

Frequently asked questions

We're a 25-office DSO on Denticon. Can Aria run for all of us at once?
Yes — but most large groups prefer a phased rollout. We typically start with 2–3 pilot offices for the first 30 days, validate the routing rules and call patterns, then onboard the rest in waves. The infrastructure is the same regardless of group size; the rollout cadence is what changes.
Can Aria handle our existing Denticon-native call routing rules?
Aria's routing layer sits in front of any per-office call flows you already have. We can match your existing escalation patterns (transfer to billing, transfer to office manager, route emergencies to on-call) on a per-office or group-wide basis.
Does Aria conflict with Planet DDS's Patient Engagement tools?
No. If you already use Planet DDS's reminder or two-way text engine, Aria's reminders can be turned off so you don't double-message patients. Voice and inbound chat work the same regardless.
What does Aria cost at DSO scale?
Pricing scales with locations and call volume, not per-seat. Talk to us about your office count and rough monthly call volume and we'll quote a group rate. Most multi-location DSOs see lower per-office Aria spend than single-location practices because the platform overhead is shared.
Is Aria HIPAA-compliant for DSO use?
Yes. We sign a BAA with the operating entity (DSO or MSO) and apply per-location data scoping. PHI is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and in transit with TLS. See /security for the full posture.
