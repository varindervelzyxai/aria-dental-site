Dental practice landscape in Illinois

What's unique about practicing dentistry in Illinois.

Illinois has roughly 9,000 active dentists per ADA membership data. The Chicago metro accounts for the majority of practice volume; downstate practice density is significantly lower.

Delta Dental of Illinois, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois (HCSC), Cigna, MetLife, Humana, and Guardian are the dominant commercial carriers. Illinois Medicaid (HFS) and managed-care plans cover a meaningful share of pediatric and low-income adult dental.

Illinois has moderate DSO penetration. Heartland Dental is headquartered in Effingham (downstate Illinois), which gives the state outsized DSO infrastructure. Aspen Dental and Pacific Dental Services also have Illinois footprints.

Illinois has a 17% Hispanic/Latino population, with higher concentration in Chicago, suburban Cook County, and certain downstate communities (Aurora, Cicero, Berwyn). Polish and Mandarin are also common patient languages in some Chicago neighborhoods.

Illinois compliance considerations

State-specific compliance, plain English.

Federal HIPAA is the floor. Illinois layers additional rules on top. The compliance areas every Illinois practice should think about when evaluating any AI vendor.

•

Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA)

BIPA is the strictest biometric law in the U.S. — it requires written consent before collecting any biometric identifier, including voice patterns. Voice-biometrics-based AI receptionists (which Aria does NOT do — Aria identifies callers by phone-hash + name match, not voiceprints) would be in BIPA scope.

•

Illinois Personal Information Protection Act

PIPA requires breach notification 'without unreasonable delay,' with specific notification content. Stricter than federal in some respects.

•

HIPAA + Illinois Mental Health and Developmental Disabilities Confidentiality Act

Illinois layers additional confidentiality protections on top of federal HIPAA for mental-health-adjacent records.

•

Telehealth Improvement Act (effective 2022)

Illinois has expanded telehealth rules that affect how AI-collected intake flows when teledentistry is part of the workflow. Important for orthodontic, ortho-tied screening, and specialty practices.
