Workflows

Why add Aria to Carestream?

Carestream's strength is in clinical and imaging workflows. The front office layer — particularly after-hours coverage, insurance verification time, and chat — is where most Carestream practices report friction. Aria addresses that without asking you to change clinical workflows.

Frequently asked questions

Does Aria work with both SoftDent and OrthoTrac?
Yes. Both are Carestream products and follow the same integration path through Sikka. Specialty-specific workflows for OrthoTrac (banding, debanding, retainer recall) are on the Q4 roadmap.
What if my practice is on Carestream's older product?
Voice + chat still work day-one. PMS-deep features depend on API access; we'll evaluate fit during the demo.
Can Aria coexist with Carestream's reminder system?
Yes — turn Aria's reminders off if Carestream's reminders are already running. Voice + chat are unaffected.
Is Aria HIPAA-compliant for Carestream practices?
Yes. We sign a BAA, encrypt all PHI at rest (AES-256-GCM), and use HIPAA-aligned model providers. See the security page.
Outcomes

What practices on Aria + Carestream typically see.

Carestream practices on Aria typically see the most measurable gains in three specific areas in the first 90 days. First, after-hours call coverage goes from voicemail to 100% answered. Honest comparison

How Aria differs from generic AI receptionists.

Carestream offers some first-party messaging and reminder products, but those tools don't include AI voice answering or real-time insurance eligibility. Aria fills both gaps. The honest comparison is: if you're happy with Carestream's clinical and imaging tools, you don't need to migrate — you just need to layer Aria on top of the front office, and let Carestream continue to do what it does best on the clinical side.
