1. Insurance Verification

Why this matters: Aria reads back annual maximum, deductible, preventive coverage, and exam frequency in 90 seconds. Front-desk staff used to do this manually after the call — if they remembered.

PatientDaniel Reyes.
AriaThanks Daniel. Date of birth?
PatientOctober seventeenth, nineteen eighty-five.
AriaAnd the member ID from your insurance card?
PatientB-T-nine-four-two-seven-five-one.
AriaInsurance carrier?
PatientBlueCross BlueShield.
AriaGot it. You're confirmed under BlueCross BlueShield PPO. Your annual maximum is twenty-five hundred dollars, and your deductible is fifty dollars — none of it met yet this year.
AriaFor preventive — cleanings, exams, and x-rays — your plan covers one hundred percent, no deductible. Basic services like fillings are at eighty percent after deductible, and major services like crowns or root canals at fifty percent.
PatientHow many cleanings am I covered for?
AriaTwo cleanings per year, six months apart. You're also covered for two routine exams annually, one set of bitewing x-rays per year, and a full panoramic x-ray every three years.
