The Multi-Location Problem: Inconsistent Experience, Unscalable Hiring

Every location needs a front desk. Every front desk needs training. Every trained receptionist eventually leaves — and you start over.

Across 3, 5, 10+ locations, the compounding cost is brutal. The quality problem is worse: one location answers every call with empathy and precision. Another holds patients for three minutes. A third sends everything to voicemail after lunch.

You can't standardize human performance across locations. You can standardize AI.

Hiring doesn't scale

$70K per receptionist per location. Multiply by your office count. Add turnover, training, sick days, and inconsistency.

Quality is inconsistent

Your best location answers every call perfectly. Your worst sends patients to voicemail. You can't be everywhere at once.

Reporting is fragmented

Call volume, booking rates, missed calls, insurance checks — scattered across locations with no unified view.

Frequently Asked Questions
Multi-location questions.
"Can Aria work across multiple locations?"
Yes. Aria supports multi-location deployment with per-location configuration — different providers, schedules, insurance preferences, and voice personality — all managed from a single centralized dashboard.
"Does each location get its own configuration?"
Yes. Each office gets its own provider routing, scheduling rules, hours, insurance preferences, and branded voice personality. Patients calling one location never hear another location's branding or scheduling.
"Is patient data isolated between locations?"
Yes. Strict data isolation between practices for HIPAA compliance. Each location's patient data, conversations, and insurance records are completely separated. Centralized reporting shows aggregate performance without exposing individual patient records. Read more about security →
"How does this compare to hiring per location?"
A receptionist costs ~$70K/year per location. Across 5 locations, that's $350K — before turnover, training, sick days, and inconsistency. Aria provides 24/7 coverage across all locations at a fraction of that cost with consistent quality. Run your numbers →
"Does Aria integrate with our PMS?"
Aria integrates with Google Calendar and Stripe today. OpenDental launches April 2026. Dentrix and Eaglesoft via Sikka are on the roadmap. PMS integration depth is scoped per location during onboarding.
"How quickly can we add new locations?"
New locations onboard in 1–2 weeks. Because Aria is software, adding a location requires no hiring, training, or hardware. Configuration is duplicated from existing locations and customized for the new office.
