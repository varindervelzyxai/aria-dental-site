Specialty workflow

Where pediatric dental practices (pillar) break standard AI front office.

Generic AI receptionists treat every dental call like a cleaning request. The specialty workflows below are where they fall apart — and what we built Aria to handle.

•

Parent calls, child receives care

Pediatric front office is unique: the patient calling is almost never the patient receiving care. Aria handles the parent fluently — captures parent name, child's name, child's age, child's insurance (often via parent), and books for the child.

•

School-friendly scheduling

Parents prefer late-afternoon and Saturday slots. Aria knows your school-friendly slots and prioritizes them. Won't book a 10 a.m. cleaning if the child has school.

•

First-visit anxiety + parent worry

First dental visits for kids carry anxiety for both child and parent. Aria's voice template uses warm, kid-friendly language; explains what the first visit looks like in 1–2 sentences; and reassures parents who ask 'what should I tell my child.'

•

Insurance + Medicaid handling

Many pediatric practices accept state Medicaid (Denti-Cal in CA, Medicaid in others). Aria's insurance flow handles both private dental and Medicaid plan IDs without making either feel like the 'second-class' payer.

•

Sibling appointment coordination

Parents often want to bring 2-3 kids in the same visit. Aria can book sibling appointments back-to-back with the same provider when possible — reducing parent waiting room time.

•

Sedation-pediatric workflows

For practices that do nitrous or oral sedation on pediatric cases, Aria can read the pre-op instructions, confirm escort plan, and SMS-reminds the day before.

Scheduling

Specialty-aware scheduling.

Aria's scheduling engine knows the difference between a 30-minute cleaning and a 90-minute multi-quadrant case. For pediatric dental practices (pillar), that includes:

  • → School-friendly slots highlighted in the available-times response.
  • → Sibling booking: Aria can book 2 siblings back-to-back with the same provider.
  • → Age-appropriate appointment lengths: 30-min for ages 3-5, 45-min for first visits.

What success looks like

Concrete outcomes.

Success for a pediatric practice on Aria looks like: every parent calls and reaches a warm, kid-friendly voice that asks the right questions about the child without making the parent repeat their information three times. School-friendly slots get prioritized in the available-times response. First-visit anxiety conversations get handled with appropriate language for both the parent and (when on speaker) the child. Sibling appointments get coordinated automatically. Medicaid + private dental plans get verified with the same speed and dignity.

The objection we hear

"But our specialty is too complex for AI."

The most common objection from pediatric practices is 'we worry parents will be put off by AI.' We share that concern — pediatric front office is the most emotionally sensitive part of the dental industry. Aria's pediatric voice template is the most carefully tuned of all our templates. It uses warm, parent-friendly language; doesn't try to be funny; explains what to expect at the first visit in 1-2 sentences without creating anxiety; and escalates clinical questions to your team rather than fabricating answers. Most pediatric practices on Aria report the parent feedback has been overwhelmingly positive — but we'd rather you hear that from references during your demo than take our word for it.
