Aria + Dentrix Ascend

Aria for Dentrix Ascend. Cloud-native AI front office for cloud-native dentistry.

Built for Dentrix Ascend's modern API. OAuth-secured, real-time, no on-prem agents required.

Book a Demo →See How It Works

Dentrix Ascend is Henry Schein's cloud rewrite of Dentrix — and the difference matters. Where the on-prem Dentrix integration depends on Sikka or eDex middleware to surface data, Ascend exposes a modern OAuth 2.0 REST API that makes Aria's job dramatically cleaner. No on-prem agent. No screen-scraping. No license keys mailed in shrink-wrap. We authenticate once, refresh tokens on the standard schedule, and call the same endpoints your front desk's browser does. That's why our Ascend integration ships ahead of legacy-Dentrix write-back even though Dentrix has been on the market longer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dentrix Ascend the same as Dentrix?
No. Dentrix is the on-prem product Henry Schein has sold for decades — your data lives on a server in your office. Dentrix Ascend is the cloud-native product they built from scratch, with a modern web UI and a REST API. The two share a name and a parent company, but the integration mechanics are completely different. Aria has separate adapters for each.
Do I need a Henry Schein partner agreement to enable this?
For the API scopes Aria needs, an Ascend admin in your practice can grant access directly. For some advanced endpoints, Henry Schein requires a partner-signed app review — we handle that on our side. The customer flow is unchanged either way: an admin clicks Authorize.
What about Dentrix Enterprise (the DSO version)?
Dentrix Enterprise is yet a third product — Henry Schein's on-prem DSO platform. Our Dentrix adapter (the Sikka-based one) covers Enterprise as well. Tell us your specific deployment on the demo and we'll confirm the right adapter.
Can Aria run for a multi-location group on Ascend?
Yes. Ascend's tenant + location model maps cleanly to Aria's multi-location routing. We can route inbound calls to the correct location based on the caller's history, voice prompt, or DID. Production hardening for large DSO deployments lands Q1 2027.
What does it cost to add Aria on top of Dentrix Ascend?
Aria pricing is independent of PMS. Most single-location practices land in the $499–$699/month range, all-in. Multi-location DSO pricing is volume-based — talk to us.
