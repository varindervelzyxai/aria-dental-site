Emergency Routing and Triage

🚨

Aria detects emergencies — and routes them instantly.

When a caller mentions severe pain, swelling, trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, or a knocked-out tooth, Aria recognizes the urgency immediately. Instead of booking an appointment, Aria routes the call to your designated on-call number or emergency contact — ensuring the patient reaches a real person within seconds.

Aria never provides clinical advice. It ensures the patient reaches the right person fast. Every emergency routing is logged and visible in your admin dashboard the next morning.
