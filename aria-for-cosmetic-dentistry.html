Specialty workflow

Where cosmetic dentistry practices break standard AI front office.

Generic AI receptionists treat every dental call like a cleaning request. The specialty workflows below are where they fall apart — and what we built Aria to handle for cosmetic dentistry practices.

•

Elective patients ask different questions

A cosmetic patient calling about veneers asks different questions than a patient calling about a cleaning — about case examples, before/after results, smile-design process, financing. Aria's voice and chat templates for cosmetic practices are tuned to those conversations.

•

Higher per-case value, longer close cycle

A cosmetic case can be $5,000–$50,000+. Patients shop. Aria handles the multi-touch courtship — first inquiry, consult, follow-up after consult, before-and-after sharing, financing conversation — without losing them to a competitor's faster response.

•

Smile-design consultation booking

Cosmetic consults are longer and more involved than a standard new-patient appointment. Aria books the right duration with the right cosmetic dentist (when split across providers) and sends a pre-consult intake link to capture goals, photos, and expectations.

•

Anxious aesthetic patients

Cosmetic patients are often more anxious than the average new patient — they're investing significant money in how they'll look. Aria handles the anxiety-driven 'will it hurt? will it look natural? what if I don't like it?' questions with empathy, not pressure.

•

Photo and case-example workflow

Many cosmetic patients want to see case examples before committing. Aria can route case-example requests to your treatment coordinator and follow up with a curated set of relevant before/afters by SMS or email.

Scheduling

Specialty-aware scheduling.

Cosmetic scheduling is about the right amount of time with the right provider, plus consistent pre-consult preparation. Aria's scheduling logic for cosmetic practices reflects that.

  • → Cosmetic consults: 60–90 min with the cosmetic dentist (not a hygienist).
  • → Veneer prep: 2–4 hours, restorative provider, lab-coordination day.
  • → Bonding/whitening: shorter slots, often hygienist or assistant-led.
  • → Photo / smile-design imaging: pre-consult appointment with photographer or imaging tech.

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQ

Frequently asked questions for cosmetic dentistry practices.

Can Aria handle the 'I want a Hollywood smile' opening question?

Yes. Aria's cosmetic templates are tuned to convert exploratory cosmetic interest into a booked consultation, without overpromising results. The clinical conversation about what's actually achievable for the patient stays with the cosmetic dentist.

How does Aria handle financing conversations?

Aria explains the financing options your practice offers (CareCredit, Sunbit, Cherry, in-house financing). It doesn't run a credit application — that happens in person or through a link sent post-call.

Does Aria send case examples?

On request, Aria can route the patient to your treatment coordinator with a structured handoff (what they're interested in, their goals, their timeline). Your team curates and sends the actual before/afters via SMS or email.

Can Aria handle Invisalign / clear aligner inquiries?

Yes — Invisalign is a common cosmetic-adjacent inquiry. Aria books the right consult (with Invisalign-trained provider) and sets pre-visit expectations for the iTero scan.
