Specialty workflow

Where implant dentistry practices break standard AI front office.

Generic AI receptionists treat every dental call like a cleaning request. The specialty workflows below are where they fall apart — and what we built Aria to handle for implant dentistry practices.

•

Long case-acceptance arc

Implant cases close in 6–18 months from first inquiry to chair time. Aria handles the multi-touch arc — first call, consultation booking, follow-up after consultation, second-opinion conversations — without losing patients to silence.

•

Surgical pre-op anxiety

Patients ask the same 8–10 pre-op questions in the days before surgery. Aria handles them warmly, escalates the genuine clinical questions, and only interrupts the surgeon for what actually requires a doctor's voice.

•

Insurance-then-financing reality

Most implant cases are partially covered (depending on plan), so the conversation becomes 'insurance covers X, you'd be responsible for Y.' Aria explains the math without pressure, offers financing options, and routes to your team for case acceptance.

•

Referring GP handoffs

Many implant cases come from GP referrals. Aria captures the referring doctor, scope of referral, and patient urgency in a structured handoff — critical for tracking ROI on referral relationships.

•

Multi-stage scheduling

Single-tooth implant ≠ full-arch ≠ All-on-X. Aria knows the duration and provider type for each stage and schedules accordingly.

Scheduling

Specialty-aware scheduling.

Aria's scheduling engine knows the difference between an implant consult, a surgical placement, an abutment connection, and a crown delivery. Each gets the right duration, the right provider, and the right pre-op communication.

  • → Provider-aware: separate scheduling logic for implant surgeon vs. restorative dentist (when split across providers).
  • → Duration-aware: 60-min consult ≠ 90-min single-tooth surgery ≠ 4-hour full-arch.
  • → Surgical block protection: Aria respects surgical days and won't book a hygiene cleaning into surgical time.
  • → Sedation pre-op: when IV sedation is part of the case, Aria sends the standard pre-op instructions automatically.

Implant Dentistry FAQ

Frequently asked questions for implant dentistry practices.

Can Aria handle case acceptance conversations?

Aria handles the front-office portion of case acceptance — explaining what insurance covers, what the patient is responsible for, financing options, and scheduling. The clinical case-acceptance conversation (treatment plan, surgical approach, expected outcomes) stays with your team. We don't fake clinical authority.

How does Aria handle implant emergencies (failed implant, post-op infection)?

Aria triages by symptom severity using your practice's protocol, schedules an urgent visit if available, and escalates to a clinical team member if the symptoms suggest something serious. Pain-empathy first (Rule #17 in our front-office playbook), booking second.

Does Aria support full-arch / All-on-X workflows?

Yes — full-arch cases are scheduled as multi-visit treatment plans with the right provider for each stage. The case-acceptance conversation typically routes to your treatment coordinator, not Aria.

How do referring GPs hand off to Aria?

Two paths. (1) Phone call to your office — Aria captures the referring doctor's name, scope of referral, and patient urgency, and surfaces it to your team. (2) Web form referral — your referring GPs submit through your referral portal; Aria contacts the patient by phone within 24 hours to schedule consult.
