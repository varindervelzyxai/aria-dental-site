AI Chat Widget

Embeds on your website in one line of code. Answers patient questions 24/7 using AI trained on your practice. Captures leads and books appointments directly — no forms, no phone tag.

📱

AI Phone Agent

Answers every call with a natural voice. Handles scheduling, FAQs, and routing simultaneously. Never puts a patient on hold. Never sends them to voicemail. Even after hours.

👋

Returning Patient Recognition

Aria recognizes returning patients by phone number before they say a word. Previous visit history, last service, and insurance records load automatically. The experience feels personal from the first second.

📅

Smart Scheduling

Patients book directly through chat or phone. Real-time availability from your calendar. Automatic conflict detection. Confirmation emails and reminders sent instantly.

🔄

Multi-Appointment Booking

Patients often need more than one service. "I need a cleaning and a filling." Aria handles this naturally — checking availability for each service with the right provider, booking both, and sending one combined confirmation.

🖥️

In-Office Kiosk

Tablet-based check-in for walk-ins and appointments. QR code scanning. Digital intake forms. Zero paper. Zero wait. Insurance verified before they reach the chair.

🛡️

Real-Time Insurance Verification

The only AI receptionist that verifies insurance during a live phone call. Aria collects the member ID and date of birth, checks eligibility across 3,400+ payers, and reads back coverage — deductibles, coinsurance, remaining annual maximum — all before the patient hangs up. Works for dependents too. Learn more →

Payment Collection

Collect co-pays, deposits, and balances through chat, kiosk, or automated follow-up texts. Stripe-powered. PCI compliant. Patients pay on their terms.
