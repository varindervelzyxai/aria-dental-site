A virtual receptionist for the front desk

Aria is the AI voice receptionist from Aria Dental, a product of Velzyx AI Inc. in California. For a dental clinic in Dubai, a virtual receptionist answers when the front desk is with a patient, at lunch, after close or on a weekend. It takes appointment requests, captures the details the clinic wants, and hands anything clinical or urgent to the team. It supports your staff. It doesn't replace them. Each Aria is custom-built for the clinic.

We're taking discovery calls with dental clinics in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and across the UAE. We aren't live in the UAE yet, so nothing on this page is a result, a testimonial, or a claim that Aria already answers a Dubai clinic's line. There is no UAE number and no Dubai office.

Dental call answering, and what Aria will not do

Dental call answering here means the calls the desk cannot pick up. Aria takes appointment requests and the details the clinic asks for. It doesn't give clinical advice, diagnose or triage. Urgent calls are routed under rules the clinic sets.

Practice software we already work with (Open Dental, Dentrix and Eaglesoft) shows the kind of integration work we do. A UAE clinic's system would be scoped on the call, not assumed. Checking whether a crown is ready, or moving a visit earlier, depends on that software being connected.

After-hours dental calls and local rules

A useful first conversation starts from the clinic's own hours: Saturday openings, Friday half-days, after-hours dental calls in Dubai, and the questions patients actually ask. The same conversation applies to a clinic in Abu Dhabi or Sharjah. UAE insurance doesn't work like the US eligibility checks described elsewhere on this site, and we won't pretend it does. If insurance questions are in scope, they'd be answered only from information the clinic approves, or routed to the team.

The public voice library is English, Spanish, Chinese and Hindi.

How it would work for your clinic

Aria is not live in the UAE. This is the plan for a future deployment, after a discovery call. It is not a result from a Dubai clinic.

  1. 1

    Discovery call about your hours

    We start from the week you actually run: Saturday openings, Friday half-days, lunch, and what should happen after close. The same questions apply in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.

  2. 2

    We custom-build Aria for your clinic

    Services, the questions to ask, what to write down, and the rules for handing a call to a person. Built for that clinic, not a shared script.

  3. 3

    Aria answers overflow and after-hours calls

    When the desk is with a patient, or the clinic is closed, Aria answers, takes the appointment request, and saves the details for your team to confirm.

  4. 4

    Anything clinical or urgent goes to your team

    Aria does not give advice. Pain, swelling, and anything you mark as urgent is transferred or routed to your team under the rules you set.

Example calls

Three short samples of how a future deployment could sound. Each one is an example call, illustrative, not a real clinic. They are not recordings, and they are not results.

Example call (illustrative, not a real clinic)

Friday afternoon, after the clinic closes

Friday afternoon. In this example the clinic closed at 1pm for a Friday half-day. The caller wants a cleaning next week.

Caller

I'd like to book a cleaning for next week. Are you still open today?

Aria

The clinic is closed for the rest of Friday. I can take a cleaning request for next week and send the details to the team to confirm. Which day works, and may I take your name?

Caller

Tuesday morning. You can use the number I'm calling from.

Aria

I have a cleaning request for Tuesday morning, with a callback on this number. The team confirms the time. I can't set it as a final booking while the clinic is closed.

Example call (illustrative, not a real clinic)

Is the crown ready, and can the visit move up?

The caller asks about a crown and an earlier visit. Aria checks only because this sample clinic's software is connected.

Caller

Is my crown ready, and can I come in sooner than Thursday?

Aria

I can check that because this clinic's software is connected. The crown is marked ready, and there is an earlier opening Wednesday at 10. I can request that move. The team still confirms it.

Caller

Wednesday at 10 works.

Aria

I've requested Wednesday at 10 and noted that the crown is marked ready. Someone at the clinic will confirm.

Aria can check crown status and offer an earlier visit in Dentrix, Eaglesoft or Open Dental when that software is connected. If it is not connected, Aria does not guess. It takes the question for the team.

Example call (illustrative, not a real clinic)

Severe swelling and pain

The caller reports severe swelling and pain. Aria does not advise. It routes the call under the clinic's rules.

Caller

My face is swollen and it hurts a lot. What should I do?

Aria

I can't give dental or medical advice. Severe swelling and pain go to the clinic team right away, under this clinic's rules. I'm transferring you now.

The call is routed to the team. Aria does not diagnose, suggest treatment, or decide whether the caller should wait.
