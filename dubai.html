Built for the front desk

Aria is the AI voice receptionist from Aria Dental, a product of Velzyx AI Inc. in California. It answers when the front desk is with a patient, at lunch, after close or on a weekend. It takes appointment requests, captures the details the clinic wants, and hands anything clinical or urgent to the team. It supports your staff; it doesn't replace them. Each Aria is custom-built for the clinic, not one-size-fits-all.

We're expanding to the UAE and taking discovery calls with dental clinics in Dubai and across the Emirates. We aren't live in the UAE yet, so nothing on this page is a result, a testimonial or a claim that Aria already answers a Dubai clinic's line.

What Aria handles on a dental line, and what it won't do

It takes appointment requests, captures the details the clinic wants, and hands anything clinical or urgent to the team.

Aria doesn't give clinical advice, diagnose or triage. Urgent calls are routed under rules the clinic sets.

Practice software we already work with (Open Dental, Dentrix and Eaglesoft) shows the kind of integration work we do; a UAE clinic's system would be scoped on the call, not assumed.

Languages, insurance and local rules, stated plainly

A useful first conversation starts from the clinic's own hours and setup: Saturday openings, Friday half-days, after-hours emergencies, and the questions patients actually ask. UAE insurance doesn't work like the US eligibility checks described elsewhere on this site, and we won't pretend it does. If insurance questions are in scope, they'd be answered only from information the clinic approves, or routed to the team.

The public voice library is English, Spanish, Chinese and Hindi.

Frequently asked questions

Is Aria already answering calls for dental clinics in Dubai?

No. We're expanding to the UAE and taking discovery calls. We don't have UAE clinic clients, a UAE phone number or a Dubai office yet.

Will Aria replace our receptionist?

No. Aria supports the front desk with overflow, after-hours and busy moments. Your team stays.

Does Aria give clinical advice?

No. Clinical questions and emergencies go to the clinic's team under rules set with the clinic.

Do you support Arabic, WhatsApp or a UAE number?

Not today. Arabic isn't in the public voice library, WhatsApp isn't a channel we offer, and we don't have a UAE number. We can talk about future needs on a discovery call.

Are you certified by UAE health authorities or hosted in the UAE?

No. We don't claim DHA, DoH or UAE data-residency approval. If that matters for a future deployment, we'll cover it on the call.
