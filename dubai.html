What Aria does

Aria is the AI voice receptionist from Aria Dental, a product of Velzyx AI Inc. It covers the line so your front desk can stay with the patient in front of them.

  • Answers every call

    When the front desk is busy, at lunch, after close and on weekends.

  • Books, moves and confirms

    Aria books, moves and confirms appointment requests.

  • Checks the schedule

    When connected to Dentrix, Eaglesoft or Open Dental, Aria can check whether a crown is ready and offer an earlier visit.

  • Answers common questions

    Hours, directions as the clinic provides them, services and new-patient info, from what the clinic approves.

  • Sends the team a summary

    Aria captures the details and sends your team a summary of the call.

  • Transfers the call that needs a person

    Anything clinical, urgent or complex goes straight to your front desk.

  • Built for your clinic

    Each Aria is custom-built for that clinic. Not one-size-fits-all.

  • Supports your staff

    Aria supports the front desk. It doesn't replace them.

Built around your clinic's hours

Saturday openings, Friday half-days, lunch and after close are part of the setup. Aria follows the week your clinic actually runs, in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah.

Aria speaks English, Spanish, Chinese and Hindi.

A day at the front desk

How Aria works with your clinic

  1. 1

    We start from your hours

    Saturday openings, Friday half-days, lunch, and what should happen after close. The same setup in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.

  2. 2

    We custom-build Aria for your clinic

    Services, the questions to ask, what to write down, and the rules for handing a call to a person. Built for that clinic, not a shared script.

  3. 3

    Aria answers overflow and after-hours calls

    When the desk is with a patient, or the clinic is closed, Aria answers, takes the appointment request, and sends your team the details.

  4. 4

    Anything clinical or urgent goes straight to your team

    Pain, swelling, and anything you mark as urgent is transferred to your front desk under the rules you set.

Example calls

Example call

Friday afternoon, after the clinic closes

Friday afternoon, after a 1pm close. The caller wants a cleaning next week.

Caller

I'd like to book a cleaning for next week. Are you still open today?

Aria

The clinic is closed for the rest of Friday. I can take a cleaning request for next week and send the details to the team. Which day works, and may I take your name?

Caller

Tuesday morning. You can use the number I'm calling from.

Aria

I have a cleaning request for Tuesday morning, with a callback on this number. The team will confirm the time.

Example call

Is the crown ready, and can the visit move up?

The caller asks whether a crown is ready and if the visit can move earlier.

Caller

Is my crown ready, and can I come in sooner than Thursday?

Aria

Your crown is marked ready, and there is an earlier opening Wednesday at 10. I can request that move. The team will confirm it.

Caller

Wednesday at 10 works.

Aria

I've requested Wednesday at 10 and noted that the crown is marked ready. Someone at the clinic will confirm.

When Dentrix, Eaglesoft or Open Dental is connected, Aria can check whether a crown is ready and offer an earlier visit.

Severe swelling and pain

Severe swelling and pain go straight to the clinic team. I'm transferring you now.
