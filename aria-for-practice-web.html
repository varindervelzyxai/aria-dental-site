Frequently asked questions

Will Aria work with Practice-Web's billing system?
Aria handles patient-facing payments via Stripe (co-pays, deposits, balances by SMS link). Practice-Web continues to handle ledger and insurance billing.
Is voice + chat enough without PMS sync?
For most practices, yes — most missed-call revenue comes from coverage hours, not from PMS depth. We'd rather be honest about the integration roadmap than overstate today's capabilities.
Can my staff still use Practice-Web normally?
Absolutely. Aria runs alongside, doesn't touch the Practice-Web database, doesn't require any Practice-Web changes.
How will the deep integration be priced when it ships?
Same pricing model. PMS depth doesn't change Aria's per-practice subscription cost.
Outcomes

What practices on Aria + Practice Web typically see.

Practice-Web practices on Aria see the biggest gains where they need them most: after-hours coverage moving from ~10% answered to 100%, insurance verification from days to seconds (independent of the PMS entirely), and patient communication via SMS and chat that doesn't require buying additional software licenses.
