WizKids Dental: Aria as the front line, not overflow

WizKids Dental routes every patient call to Aria first — 24/7, during AND after hours. Aria handles the call end-to-end and transfers to staff only when patient needs require human handoff. This isn't an overflow deployment. Aria is the primary receptionist.

The practice runs on Open Dental with full Aria two-way integration in production: voice agent answering every inbound call, chat widget on the website, SMS reminders, paired pediatric + ortho appointment scheduling, and dependent insurance verification all flowing through Aria. The front-desk team handles in-person patient care and the calls Aria escalates — not the routing work AI does well.
