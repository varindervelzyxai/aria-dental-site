Workflows

Why add Aria to Eaglesoft?

Eaglesoft is the front-office system you trained on. Aria isn't asking you to change that. We sit in front and handle the work that doesn't need to happen inside Eaglesoft — answering calls, verifying insurance, qualifying chat leads — and feed the right data back to your team.

Frequently asked questions

Does Aria require Patterson Connected?
For deep integration, yes. For voice + chat alone (no PMS sync), no — you can use Aria as a standalone front office layer over Eaglesoft.
What if our Patterson API access is limited?
We've worked around that with Sikka Software as the data bridge. Tell us your current setup on the demo.
How does this compare to Patterson's Fuse?
Fuse is Patterson's modern cloud platform; Aria is a different product category (AI receptionist + insurance + chat). They can coexist. Many practices on Eaglesoft today aren't on Fuse, and Aria works for both.
Will Aria break our Eaglesoft updates?
No. Aria is API-mediated — we don't touch the local database. Patterson updates deploy as normal.
Outcomes
