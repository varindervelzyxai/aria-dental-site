Workflows

Why add Aria to Curve?

Curve solved the cloud-PMS problem for clinical workflows. Aria solves the always-on receptionist problem for the front office. The two are complementary — Curve continues to be your clinical and operational source of truth, Aria handles the patient-facing communication layer.

Frequently asked questions

Does Aria work with Curve Hero on any browser?
Aria itself is independent of the browser you use to access Curve. There is no live Curve connection today.
What about Curve GRO (their messaging product)?
GRO is Curve's first-party SMS marketing tool. Aria's SMS focuses on confirmations, reminders, and reviews — different use case. Many practices run both.
Will adding Aria slow down Curve?
There is no live Curve connection today, so Aria does not add load to Curve.
Can Aria handle multi-location Curve setups?
Native Curve sync, including multi-location routing, is on the roadmap. Voice and chat do not wait on that.
Outcomes

What Aria is built to do with Curve.

Aria does not publish Curve client results. A cloud-to-cloud connection is designed to avoid on-prem integration work. Answered calls, eligibility during the visit, and chat or SMS for routine questions are product goals, not a report from practices.

Honest comparison

How Aria differs from generic AI receptionists.

Compared to Curve's first-party messaging product (Curve GRO), Aria operates in a different category — voice + insurance + booking, not SMS marketing campaigns. Aria answers inbound voice and chat, checks eligibility, and books. It does not run campaigns that call patients.
