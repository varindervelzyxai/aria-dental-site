Specialty workflow

Where endodontic practices break standard AI front office.

Generic AI receptionists treat every dental call like a cleaning request. The specialty workflows below are where they fall apart — and what we built Aria to handle.

•

Emergency intake + triage

Endo is mostly emergency or urgent care. A patient calling at 4 p.m. with a hot tooth needs to be triaged correctly: is this a same-day emergency, a next-day RCT, or a referral back to the GP? Aria asks the right qualifying questions (pain level, duration, swelling, fever, prior treatment) and surfaces the urgency to your team in seconds.

•

Pain-empathy first (Rule #17)

Aria leads with empathy when a patient mentions pain. No 'great' or 'wonderful' — just acknowledgement, then the booking. Patients in pain don't want sales energy.

•

Post-op questions

The 48-hour window after an RCT is heavy with patient questions: 'is it normal that it still hurts,' 'can I eat,' 'what about that medication.' Aria handles the standard post-op questions warmly and escalates only the questions that need your endo's voice.

•

Referral coordination with GPs

Most endo cases come from a referring GP. Aria captures the referring doctor, their case notes, the tooth number, and any treatment urgency, and packages it for your team.

•

Single-visit vs multi-visit RCT scheduling

Aria knows the difference between a single-visit RCT block (90 min) and a multi-visit case (initial + obturation). Books the correct block length and the correct gap.

Scheduling

Specialty-aware scheduling.

Aria's scheduling engine knows the difference between a 30-minute cleaning and a 90-minute multi-quadrant case. For endodontic practices, that includes:

  • → Emergency-slot-aware: Aria can hold emergency capacity and only release it within 24 hours.
  • → Block-length-aware: 60/90/120 min RCT blocks differentiate by tooth and case type.
  • → Same-day overflow handling: Aria can route after-hours emergencies to a covering endo or after-hours line.

What success looks like

Concrete outcomes.

Success for an endo practice on Aria looks like: every emergency call gets triaged correctly within 60 seconds, surfaced to your team with pain level, duration, swelling status, and prior treatment captured. After-hours emergency calls reach a covering endo (or get scheduled into the next morning's emergency slot) instead of going to voicemail. Post-op patient calls in the 48-hour window are handled warmly without requiring your endo to be on call.

The objection we hear

"But our specialty is too complex for AI."

The biggest concern endo practices raise is 'I don't want a robot triaging emergencies.' Fair concern. Aria's role isn't to replace your clinical judgment — it's to gather the right information so your team can act on it faster. The triage protocol is simple: pain level, duration, swelling, fever, prior treatment, then either schedule, transfer to a covering endo, or escalate to the on-call line. Your team configures the routing rules. Aria executes them consistently.
