Specialty workflow

Where periodontal practices break standard AI front office.

Generic AI receptionists treat every dental call like a cleaning request. The specialty workflows below are where they fall apart — and what we built Aria to handle.

•

Long treatment plans + multi-visit care

Perio patients often have 4-6 visits scoped (SRP per quadrant, re-eval, maintenance). Aria can present the full plan, schedule each visit with the appropriate hygienist or perio assistant, and explain the spacing without anxiety-driving jargon.

•

Referral intake from GPs

Most perio practices get 60–80% of their new patients via GP referral. Aria handles the referring GP's call, captures the referring doctor's name, scope of referral, and patient urgency in one structured handoff to your team.

•

Insurance verification with high coverage variance

Perio coverage is where insurance gets weird — graft codes (D4263, D4273), bone-replacement materials, biologics, frequency limitations on SRP. Aria's eligibility output flags these line by line so your team isn't surprised at check-in.

•

Surgical pre-op anxiety

Patients scheduled for crown lengthening, gum graft, or implant placement call with anxiety-driven questions in the days before. Aria handles those calls warmly, answers the standard pre-op questions, and only escalates the genuine clinical questions to your perio.

•

Pocket-depth follow-up + recall

3-month and 4-month perio maintenance recalls have higher no-show rates than hygiene. Aria's recall outreach is designed for that cadence and language.

Scheduling

Specialty-aware scheduling.

Aria's scheduling engine knows the difference between a 30-minute cleaning and a 90-minute multi-quadrant case. For periodontal practices, that includes:

  • → Provider-aware: separate calendars for perio, hygienist, and perio assistant.
  • → Procedure-time-aware: SRP per quadrant ≠ a 30-min cleaning; Aria knows.
  • → Surgical block protection: Aria respects surgical days and won't book hygiene against them.

What success looks like

Concrete outcomes.

What does success look like for a perio practice on Aria? In the first 90 days: every referral call from a referring GP is captured with the doctor's name, scope of referral, and patient urgency, and surfaced to your team in real time. Insurance verification on perio cases (which is where coverage gets the weirdest) is fully automated for in-network cases, and your team only touches the 15-20% with edge cases. Maintenance recall outreach hits the right cadence (3-month vs. 4-month vs. annual) without manual list management.

The objection we hear

"But our specialty is too complex for AI."

The most common objection from perio practices is 'our cases are too complex for AI.' That's true for the clinical conversation — Aria doesn't diagnose, doesn't recommend treatment, and doesn't pretend to be a perio. But the front-office work — scheduling SRP per quadrant with the right hygienist, verifying coverage on graft codes, confirming pre-op for surgical days, managing maintenance recall — is exactly the work that doesn't need a doctor's voice. Aria handles those, your team handles the clinical conversations.
