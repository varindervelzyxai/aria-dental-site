The DSO Operations Problem

The cost structure of multi-location dentistry doesn't scale linearly.

A challenge that is manageable at one location becomes existential at one hundred. Most front-office problems compound — they don't average out. A 2% inefficiency multiplied across 500 practices is a strategic problem, not an operational one.

01

Front-desk turnover at 40-60% annually becomes existential at scale

Industry-wide front-desk turnover sits between 40% and 60% per year per location. At 200 locations that is roughly 100 vacant or rotating roles at any moment, which means perpetual hiring, training, and supervision overhead — and a permanent gap between newly trained staff and policy. Aria collapses that surface area to a single configuration that does not quit.

02

Inconsistent patient experience erodes brand equity location by location

The whole point of a DSO is operational leverage on a brand. When a patient calls one location and gets warm, fast service and another and gets a voicemail at 4:30 PM, your brand is being defined by the weakest front desk you employ. Centralized AI conversation quality is the only mechanism that delivers a uniform first touch across every location.

03

HIPAA compliance variance — training drift across hundreds of front desks

HIPAA training is a per-employee obligation that compounds with turnover. With hundreds of front desks and turnover at the rates above, you are perpetually training new staff against the same policies and perpetually exposed to the gap between trained staff and untrained staff. Aria executes the same disclosure language and same data-handling rules on every call, every time, with full audit logging.

04

Insurance verification × hundreds of locations = millions in compounding labor cost

Manual eligibility verification typically takes 5 to 15 minutes per patient when done well. Multiplied across the new-patient volume of a multi-hundred-location DSO, that is thousands of staff hours per week dedicated to a workflow that is increasingly automatable. Aria verifies coverage live during the call across 3,400+ payers — patient cost calculated and read back before they hang up.

05

After-hours coverage forces a bad choice at every location

Most DSOs solve after-hours either with duplicated answering services per location or by accepting voicemail and the revenue leakage it creates. The first is expensive and inconsistent. The second is invisible until the corporate-development team starts asking why same-store new-patient growth has stalled. Aria runs 24/7 from a single configuration with no per-location operational overhead.

Enterprise FAQ

The questions that come up before contract signature.

Pulled from real enterprise discovery calls, security reviews, and procurement cycles. If your team has additional questions, our enterprise lead will join your discovery call directly.

How does pricing scale with locations?

Aria offers custom enterprise pricing with volume discounts that begin at 25 locations and deepen as you scale. We build proposals around the channels you turn on (voice, chat, SMS), expected call volume per location, the PMS integrations required, and the level of professional services support you need. Multi-year agreements with locked-in rates are standard for organizations above 100 locations. We typically deliver a custom quote within 48 hours of a discovery call.

Can we deploy in phases (pilot → regional → full)?

Yes — phased rollouts are the default approach for any deployment above 25 locations. A typical pattern is a 3-5 location pilot in weeks 1-4 to validate workflows and capture baseline KPIs, regional expansion across 25-50 locations in weeks 5-10 to refine the playbook, and full deployment of remaining locations in weeks 11-24. We do not recommend big-bang rollouts at enterprise scale. The phased approach lets us tune voice prompts, insurance routing, and PMS sync against your real call volume before broad deployment.

Do you support our custom PMS integration?

We currently ship production integrations for Open Dental (multi-database), Dentrix Enterprise, Eaglesoft, Curve, Carestream, and PracticeWorks. For DSOs running custom or in-house EHR/EMR systems we build direct API integrations as part of the enterprise statement of work. Our integration team scopes each custom build during the technical discovery phase and includes it in your proposal. Most custom integrations land within the first 90 days of contract execution.

What is the SLA for an enterprise contract?

Enterprise contracts ship with a 99.9% uptime SLA on the voice and chat layers, financially backed with service credits. Response time SLAs cover P1 incidents (15 minutes, 24/7), P2 (1 hour business), and P3 (next business day). Each enterprise customer is assigned a named CSM, an implementation lead, and an executive sponsor. SLAs are documented in the MSA and reviewed quarterly during business reviews.

How do you handle data residency requirements?

Aria runs in US-East and US-West regions by default with multi-region failover. Enterprise customers can elect single-region residency, dedicated tenancy, or — for organizations with state-specific requirements — pin call recordings and PHI to a designated region. We do not store or process patient data outside the United States. Custom residency options are documented in your DPA and validated during the security review.

What happens when we acquire a new practice mid-contract?

Acquisitions are a normal part of DSO operating reality and the contract is built for it. New locations roll onto your existing master agreement under the same per-location pricing. Onboarding for an acquired practice typically runs two to three weeks: PMS connection, voice prompt cloning from your standard playbook, staff change-management support, and a guided dry-run on real schedule data. Your CSM coordinates the rollout so M&A activity does not stall.

How does Aria handle multi-language patient populations across regions?

Aria responds in the patient's language automatically, with native fluency with conversational support across 100+ languages including Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Korean, Russian, and Arabic. There are no press-2-for-Spanish menus and no separate language queues to staff. Configuration is set at the organization level and inherited by every location, so a Texas region serving primarily Spanish-speaking patients and an Illinois region serving primarily Polish-speaking patients use the same product without additional setup or cost.

Can we sign a custom BAA, DPA, MSA — and what is the average legal review timeline?

Yes. Our procurement package includes a standard MSA, BAA, DPA, COI, and W-9 — all redlinable. We work with your legal and InfoSec teams directly through their preferred process (DocuSign, Ironclad, or markup-by-email). Median end-to-end legal and security review for an enterprise DSO sits between three and six weeks depending on internal cycle time. Our legal lead and security@ariadental.ai security contact are available for procurement calls during the review.
