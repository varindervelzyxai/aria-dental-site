Parent company

A product of Velzyx AI, which builds AI front-office platforms across 12 vertical industries.

Aria Dental AI is the AI front office for dental practices: voice, chat, and SMS, with native PMS integration starting with Open Dental. Aria handles inbound calls, books and reschedules appointments, runs real-time insurance eligibility, and collects payments — without disrupting clinical workflows. The first production deployment went live with a Southern California pediatric dental and orthodontic practice in early 2026.
