Aria vs Weave: an honest 2026 comparison

Aria is a purpose-built AI receptionist; Weave is a broad dental practice comms suite. Honest comparison on pricing, integrations, voice, security, and which to pick when.

Weave is the incumbent. They've been the default dental practice communication suite for the better part of a decade — VoIP phone, two-way SMS, payments, reviews, a phone-system-plus-CRM that thousands of practices already pay for. Aria is purpose-built for AI receptionist. The right comparison isn't "which is better in general" — it's "which is the right tool for the AI front office job."

Bias disclosure

We make Aria. We've tried to be fair — this is based on public information about Weave as of May 7, 2026 (their site, public investor disclosures, customer reports). Weave is a public company with a much larger product surface than Aria. If anything's outdated or inaccurate, email hello@ariadental.ai.

Quick verdict

If you want one vendor for VoIP phone, SMS, payments, and reviews — Weave. If you want best-in-class AI voice handling calls, verifying insurance live, and writing back to your PMS — Aria. Many of our customers run both: Weave as the phone and SMS rails, Aria as the AI receptionist that picks up when the front desk can't.

Different products solving overlapping problems

Weave is a comprehensive practice communications platform. AI receptionist functionality is one feature among many — and it's relatively new in the Weave product surface. Aria is built voice-first, AI-first, and does one job extremely well: handle inbound calls, verify insurance, and book appointments back into your PMS without a human picking up.

This means a lot of the comparison comes down to scope. Weave at its best replaces your phone system, your SMS tool, your reviews tool, and possibly your payment terminal — and adds some AI on top. Aria at its best replaces a missed-call problem and a 5pm-Friday answering-service problem with a 24/7 AI receptionist that talks to patients in their language and writes the booking into Open Dental.

Pricing approach

Aria publishes a price range and operates on monthly contracts. Weave bundles phone hardware, lines, and software in quote-based packages that vary widely by practice size and contract length. The honest read: Weave's all-in cost can be lower if you'd otherwise be paying separately for VoIP, SMS, reviews, and payments. Aria's all-in cost is lower if you already have a phone system you're happy with and just want AI receptionist coverage.

PMS integrations

Weave has been at this for a decade and has broad PMS integration coverage across most major dental practice management systems. Aria covers Open Dental, Dentrix, and Eaglesoft live; Curve and Carestream are on the roadmap. If you're on a niche PMS that Weave already supports and Aria doesn't yet — Weave wins on coverage. If you're on Open Dental and want deep AI write-back including procedure code mapping and insurance eligibility — Aria's depth is purpose-built for this.

Voice and AI quality

Aria's voice agent runs on a modern stack (GPT-4o on Retell), tuned specifically for dental call flows. We invest most of our engineering hours into voice quality. Weave's AI features are newer additions to a much larger product surface. Practices we've talked to who've trialed both consistently report Aria handles more complex flows (insurance verification mid-call, paired appointments, multi-provider routing) more reliably — but this changes as Weave invests in the area.

HIPAA & security

Both vendors will sign a BAA and encrypt PHI. Weave is a public company with significant compliance staffing. Aria is owner-operated with a documented security posture at /security — SOC 2 Type II in progress, target Q3 2026. For practices where vendor maturity is the deciding factor, Weave is the safer pick on paper. For practices that want to read the actual subprocessor list and BAA before committing, both vendors should be willing to share — push.

Setup time

Aria's setup is software-only and lives on top of your existing phone system. Median 2–7 days. Weave often involves swapping phone hardware, porting numbers, and training the team on the unified app — typically multi-week. If you need AI coverage by next month, Aria is faster. If you're rebuilding your whole comms stack anyway, Weave's longer timeline is part of a larger project.

Customer support

Weave has a documented support organization, in-app help, and a public knowledge base. Aria is owner-operated and direct: most onboarding escalations during the first 90 days route to Varinder personally. Tradeoffs: Weave is more institutional, Aria is more responsive. For multi-location DSOs that want documented SLAs, Weave's structure is more familiar.

When Weave is the better choice

You want a single vendor for phone, SMS, payments, reviews, and AI — and you're willing to swap your phone system to get there. Your practice is already on Weave and you want to consolidate rather than add another vendor. You're on a niche PMS Aria doesn't yet support. You value vendor maturity (public company, decade-old product) over speed of innovation.

When Aria is the better choice

You're happy with your current phone system and just want best-in-class AI receptionist on top of it. You're on Open Dental, Dentrix, or Eaglesoft and want deep PMS write-back. You want monthly contracts and published pricing. You want to be live in a week. You want hands-on Southern California-based support during onboarding. You want the AI front office to be the main product, not a feature among twenty.

Three scenarios

Scenario 1

Solo practice on a 6-year-old VoIP phone system that works fine. Owner just wants to stop missing 30% of new-patient calls after hours.

→ Aria. No reason to swap a working phone system.
Scenario 2

Three-location practice replacing a fragmented stack: Mitel phone, separate SMS tool, separate reviews tool, separate payments. Looking to consolidate.

→ Weave for the comms suite. Add Aria on top if AI receptionist is mission-critical.
Scenario 3

Practice already on Weave for two years. Front desk drowning. Owner asks: "Do we add Weave's AI features or bring in a specialist?"

→ Test both. Aria's depth on dental-specific flows often pulls ahead in head-to-head, but if Weave's AI hits a "good enough" bar for your call mix, the consolidation argument wins.
