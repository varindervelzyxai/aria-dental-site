What happens after you upload.

This is not a self-serve clone. Our voice engineers listen to every submission, filter the sample, and train a private voice model for your practice.

  1. 1You upload. One recording, 60 seconds is ideal. Any modern phone mic is fine.
  2. 2We review. Our team removes noise, extracts your voice fingerprint, and trains a private model.
  3. 3You preview. Within 3 business days, we email a preview of your Aria speaking a dental script in your voice.
  4. 4You approve, we deploy. Your voice ships into your live Aria. Never used for anyone else.

Recording tips

  • Quiet room. No open windows, no fans, no music.
  • Speak normally — the way you greet a patient on the phone.
  • Include your name and practice name in the recording.
  • Read one warm greeting and one confirmation line.
  • iPhone Voice Memos or Android Recorder work well.
