Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Aria-for-Dentrix and Aria-for-Open-Dental today?
Open Dental has full two-way sync — Aria writes appointments, patients, and insurance directly into OD. Dentrix has read-only sync today; bookings sit in Aria's queue for confirmation by your front desk. We're closing that gap by Q3 2026.
Will Aria conflict with Dentrix's existing reminder system (eDex, Lighthouse)?
No. Aria's reminders can be turned off if you already use eDex or Lighthouse. Voice and chat work the same regardless.
Do I need Sikka Software to use Aria?
Sikka is the cleanest path to Dentrix data, but we can also work via Dentrix Open Door for some practices. Tell us your current API setup on the demo and we'll confirm fit.
What does it cost to add Aria on top of Dentrix?
Aria pricing is independent of PMS. Most single-location practices land in the $499–$699/month range, all-in. See the pricing page or talk to us about volume.
