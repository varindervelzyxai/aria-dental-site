# Customer Expansion — Healthy-Customer Sequence

**Audience:** Customers in good standing (positive NPS, growing or steady call volume, on-time payments). Sent quarterly to top 30% of accounts.
**Goal:** Deepen the relationship — new features, peer-best-practices, second-location expansion, referral asks, advocate programs.
**Cadence:** 5 emails over a quarter, spaced ~3 weeks apart.
**From:** Account manager. Tone: helpful, peer-to-peer, never aggressive.

---

**Subject:** Three Aria features you probably haven't turned on yet
**Preview text:** Quick rundown of what shipped in Q[X] that fits your practice size and PMS.
**Send timing:** First week of new quarter

Hi [First Name],

Quick quarterly update on what's new in Aria — focused only on features that fit a practice your size, your PMS, and your specialty.

**1. Paired-appointment booking.** When a patient says "I need a cleaning and a filling," Aria now books both in one conversation, with the right gap between them, and sends one combined confirmation. Live for [your PMS].

**3. Per-provider voice templates.** If you have multiple doctors with very different patient styles (e.g. pediatric vs. adult), Aria can adopt different greeting tone per doctor. Useful for multi-doctor practices.

Want me to turn any of these on? Reply with which one and I'll have it live by end of week.

## Email 2 — Best practices from peers (T+21 days)

**Subject:** What [peer practice type] practices are doing differently
**Preview text:** Anonymized patterns from your peer cohort — what's working in the top 20%.
**Send timing:** T+21 days

Hi [First Name],

We pulled anonymized patterns from the top 20% of [pediatric / GP / specialty / etc.] practices on Aria last quarter. The differences are smaller than you'd think:

**1. They've defined more appointment types, not fewer.** Top-20% practices average 14 distinct appointment types in their setup. Bottom-50% practices average 6. Specificity → better scheduling → better revenue per slot.

**2. They turned on review-request SMS within the first 30 days.** 83% of top performers have it on. Adds 4-9 Google reviews/month on average.

**3. They have a payment-required flag for at least one appointment type.** Usually new-patient consult or cosmetic. Cuts no-shows by ~30% on those types.

**4. They check their dashboard at least 2x/week.** Not because they need to — Aria runs without them — but because the patterns surface things their team would miss.

If any of these don't match your current setup, reply and I'll send a 5-minute walkthrough on how to flip them on.

**Send timing:** T+42 days

**Pricing.** Second location is at a lower per-site rate than the first. Third+ locations are at the lowest tier. We can build you a custom multi-location plan that scales with you.

**Multi-location-specific features.** Smart routing (caller intent → right location), per-location voice (different doctors, different brand voice), per-location PMS connections.
