# Welcome / Pre-Demo Sequence

**Audience:** Practice owners and office managers who booked a demo on the calendar but haven't met yet.
**Goal:** Show up. Show up prepared. Reduce no-show, raise quality of conversation.
**Length:** 5 emails between booking and the demo.
**Voice:** Plain-language, warm, specific. One CTA per email. Sign as a real person, not "the team."

Replace `{{first_name}}`, `{{practice_name}}`, `{{demo_date}}`, `{{demo_time}}`, `{{rep_first_name}}`, `{{calendar_link}}`, and `{{reschedule_link}}` with your ESP's merge tags.

---

## Email 1 — Confirmation (sent immediately on booking)

**Subject:** You're on the calendar — {{demo_date}} at {{demo_time}}
**Preview:** Quick note on what to expect, and one thing to bring.

Hey {{first_name}},

You're on for {{demo_date}} at {{demo_time}}. {{rep_first_name}} from our team will hop on a quick Zoom — link goes out the morning of.

One thing that makes this demo more useful: have a rough sense of how many calls your practice gets in a typical week and roughly how many go to voicemail. We don't need exact numbers. A guess is fine. We'll do the math live.

If something comes up and you need to move it, here's the link: {{reschedule_link}}

See you then,
{{rep_first_name}}
Aria Dental AI

P.S. If you want to hear what Aria sounds like before we meet, there's a 90-second sample call on our site: https://www.ariadental.ai/demo

## Email 2 — What we'll cover (sent 2 days before demo)

**Subject:** What your demo will actually look like
**Preview:** No slides. No "let me share my screen for 40 minutes."

Hey {{first_name}},

Quick note before our call on {{demo_date}}.

A lot of demos are 40 minutes of slides and then 5 minutes of "any questions?" Ours is the opposite. Here's the rough shape:

- 5 min — what your front desk currently looks like (we listen)
- 10 min — Aria handles a live call as if she works at {{practice_name}}
- 10 min — your specific questions (insurance, PMS, security, escalations)
- 5 min — pricing and what live-in-48-hours actually means

If there's a specific scenario you want us to test — a tricky insurance check, a multi-location call, an after-hours emergency — reply with it and we'll set it up before the call.

— {{rep_first_name}}

## Email 3 — One-paragraph case study (sent 1 day before demo)

**Subject:** How WizKids Dental uses Aria as the front line
**Preview:** Pediatric + ortho. Aria first, every call.

Hey {{first_name}},

Sometimes it helps to see how someone actually deploys this.

WizKids Dental & Orthodontics is a pediatric + ortho practice in Southern California. Every patient call goes to Aria first — 24/7, during AND after hours. Aria handles the call end-to-end and only transfers to staff when patient needs require human handoff. This isn't an overflow deployment. Aria is the primary receptionist.

We'll publish the full case study in Q3 2026 with customer approval. Until then, the operational shape is the interesting part: AI handles the routing work AI does well, and the team focuses on chairside care and the calls Aria escalates.

That's the model we'll walk through tomorrow.

— {{rep_first_name}}

P.S. Feel free to forward this to your office manager or any partners you want on the call. The more eyes on the problem, the better.
