# Churn Prevention — Engagement-Drop Sequence

**Audience:** Customers showing churn-risk signals (call volume drop, dashboard logins drop, NPS dip, support ticket complaints).
**Goal:** Surface friction early, offer help, recover the relationship before they cancel.
**Cadence:** 4 emails over 21 days, with active CSM involvement after email 2.
**From:** Their account manager. Tone: human, curious, not panicky.

---

## Email 1 — Noticed lower engagement (T+0 from trigger)

**Subject:** Your Aria usage looks different — checking in
**Preview text:** Quick check-in. No script, no pitch. Just want to know if anything changed.
**Send timing:** T+0 from trigger (call volume drop ≥30% week-over-week sustained for 7 days, OR dashboard logins drop to zero for 14 days, OR NPS drop ≥3 points)

Hi [First Name],

Quick check-in — your Aria usage looks different from your normal pattern this week. Specifically:

- [Trigger: e.g., "Call volume is down ~38% from your usual baseline"]
- [Trigger: e.g., "No dashboard logins from your team in 12 days"]
- [Trigger: e.g., "Your last NPS response dropped from 9 to 6"]

A few things this could mean — none of which is alarming on its own:

- You're slow this week (vacation, weather, school break, etc.)
- Something on our end is broken or annoying you
- Your team's workflow changed and Aria stopped fitting

Mind if I ask which one — or if it's something else? Reply to this email or grab 15 minutes on my calendar: [Calendly link]. No script, no pitch.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** Reply or grab 15 minutes → [Calendly link]

## Email 2 — Offer help (T+5 days, if no response to email 1)

**Subject:** Anything I can help with?
**Preview text:** If something on Aria broke or stopped working for you, I'd rather know.
**Send timing:** T+5 days from trigger (only sends if no response to email 1)

Hi [First Name],

I sent a check-in last week and didn't hear back, which usually means one of two things:

1. You're heads-down and don't have time to respond — totally fine, no follow-up needed
2. Something on Aria is bothering you and you're not sure how to surface it

If it's #2, I'd really like to know. Some of the most useful improvements we've made to the product came from customers who were unhappy enough to almost leave but kind enough to tell us why first.

Specifically: is it the voice quality, the integration, the dashboard, the SMS reminders, the pricing, the support, or something else entirely? You can reply with one word and I'll take it from there.

If it's #1, no need to respond — I'll check back in 10 days.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** Reply with one word → email

---

## Email 3 — Escalate to CS leadership (T+12 days, if still no response or if response indicates real friction)

**Subject:** Stepping in personally — let's get this fixed
**Preview text:** If something on Aria isn't working for you, I want to fix it before you cancel.
**Send timing:** T+12 days from trigger (sent by Head of Customer Success, not the AM — adds escalation weight)

Hi [First Name],

I'm [CS Lead Name], head of customer success at Aria. I'm stepping in personally because two weeks of low engagement on a product like ours usually means we've broken something for you and we need to fix it.

Some context on what we can offer at this stage:

- **A 30-minute call with me** to hear what's not working (no AM, no script, just diagnostic)
- **A free month** while we fix whatever needs fixing — credited to your next billing cycle
- **A no-strings re-onboarding** — fresh setup, fresh appointment-type mapping, fresh phone forwarding test, with our most senior implementation engineer

Pick any combination, or tell me a fourth option I haven't thought of.

If you'd rather not engage and just want to leave, that's also fine — I'll wave the standard 30-day notice and credit your last month back. But I'd rather fix it than lose you.

[CS Lead Name]
Head of Customer Success, Aria Dental AI
[Direct phone] · [direct email]

**CTA:** Reply with what's broken → email or call directly

---

## Email 4 — Win-back offer (T+21 days, if cancellation request received OR no response after escalation)

**Subject:** Last note — and a real offer if you'd like to stay
**Preview text:** If we've earned a second chance, here's the structure. If not, no hard feelings.
**Send timing:** T+21 days from trigger, OR immediately upon cancellation request

Hi [First Name],

You either haven't responded for three weeks, or you've requested to cancel. Either way, this is the last note from me.

If you're already gone in your head, no problem — I won't keep emailing. Your data export will arrive within 5 business days; your number is portable back to wherever you want it.

If there's any version of this that ends with you staying, here's what I can offer:

- **3 months at 50% off** — no strings, just to give us another chance to earn it
- **Pause your account for up to 90 days** — keep your number, keep your settings, no charge
- **Switch to a custom plan** that scopes Aria's role narrower — voice-only, after-hours-only, etc., at a lower price

I'd rather keep you at half-price than lose you completely. The product gets better the longer we know what's working and what isn't, and your feedback over time is more valuable to us than the first 90 days of revenue.

If any of these land, reply or call my direct line: [phone].

If not, thank you for trying Aria. I mean that.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** Pick an offer or wave goodbye → reply
