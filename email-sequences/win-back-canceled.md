# Win-Back — Post-Cancellation Sequence

**Audience:** Customers who have officially cancelled and exited.
**Goal:** Stay in touch graciously; offer return path at the right intervals; never feel desperate.
**Cadence:** 4 emails over 90 days post-cancellation.
**From:** Account manager (the same human they worked with). Tone: respectful, low-pressure, genuinely helpful.

---

## Email 1 — Cancellation confirmation + door-open note (T+0 from cancellation)

**Subject:** Your Aria account is cancelled — and one note before we part
**Preview text:** Confirmation, your data export, and one small request.
**Send timing:** T+0 (same day as cancellation processing)

Hi [First Name],

Confirming your Aria account has been cancelled. The details:

- **Final billing date:** [date]
- **Number portability:** [reference number] — your phone provider will need this if you're porting back. We've already initiated; they'll have it in 24 hours.
- **Data export:** delivered to [email] within 5 business days. Includes all call recordings, transcripts, patient profiles, appointment history, and insurance verifications. Standard CSV/JSON formats.
- **Final dashboard access:** open until [30 days from cancellation date], read-only.

One small request before we part: would you be willing to spend 5 minutes telling me what happened? Not a survey — just a reply with what didn't work.

The reason I ask: the most useful product feedback we get comes from customers who left. We learn more from "Aria didn't fit because X" than from any feature request. If you're up for it, reply with your X.

If not, no worries. Best of luck with what comes next, and the door is always open if anything changes.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** Tell us what didn't fit → reply

## Email 2 — What's improved since you left (T+30 days)

**Subject:** A 30-day update — in case it changes anything
**Preview text:** Some things we shipped since you left. Sharing in case any of them addresses what you raised.
**Send timing:** T+30 days

Hi [First Name],

It's been 30 days since you left Aria. I wanted to share a short list of things we've shipped or changed in that month — focused only on areas that overlapped with the feedback you (or customers like you) gave us.

**Recent improvements:**

- [Item that addresses the cancellation reason — e.g., "Two-way Dentrix sync via Sikka — your specific pain point"]
- [Item — e.g., "Reduced first-response latency from 1.2s to 0.7s on voice"]
- [Item — e.g., "New per-provider voice templates — you mentioned wanting this"]
- [Item — e.g., "Multi-location pricing dropped 40%"]

This isn't a pitch to come back. It's a "you should know what changed" because some customers leave when a specific thing isn't ready and assume it'll never be ready. Sometimes it is, sometimes it isn't.

If any of the above is interesting, reply or grab 15 minutes here: [Calendly]. If not, no follow-up.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** Tell me more → reply or [Calendly link]

## Email 3 — Reduced-rate return offer (T+60 days)

**Subject:** A returning-customer offer — open through [date]
**Preview text:** If Aria looks different now than it did 60 days ago, here's a reduced rate to try again.
**Send timing:** T+60 days

Hi [First Name],

Quick one — for customers who left in the last 60 days, we have a returning-customer offer that's open through [date 30 days from send].

The offer:

- **First 3 months at 50% off** ($249/month vs. $499)
- **No setup fee** — your previous configuration (appointment types, voice template, integration) is still in your account in cold storage. We can have you back live in 24 hours.
- **Direct line to me** for the first 30 days — text or call, daily check-ins if you want them.
- **30-day exit clause** — if it's not better than last time, walk away in 30 days with a prorated refund.

What changed on our side that justifies the offer:

- [List 2-3 specific things shipped since they left, especially ones that map to their cancellation reason]
- [E.g., "Open Dental write-back is now production-stable"]
- [E.g., "Spanish-language voice quality up 30%"]

If you're tempted, reply with one word — anything — and I'll set up the re-onboarding call.

If not, this is the last reduced-rate offer you'll see from us. After this, we'd ask you to come back at the regular rate.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** I'm interested → reply

## Email 4 — Final check-in (T+90 days)

**Subject:** Last note from me — and a thank-you
**Preview text:** This is the last email you'll get from Aria unless you come back to us.
**Send timing:** T+90 days

Hi [First Name],

This is the last email you'll get from Aria. After this, we close the file. If you ever want to come back, just email me directly at [AM email] — I'll be here.

A few things before I sign off:

**Thank you.** Onboarding a new vendor is a real ask, and you took it. Whether it worked out or didn't, we're better at this because of customers like you.

**Your data is yours.** If you ever need historical call recordings, transcripts, or patient profiles from your time with Aria, we hold them encrypted for 24 months from your cancellation date. Just email me and I'll pull them.

**The door stays open.** If your situation changes — different PMS, different practice volume, different specialty focus — and you want to take a fresh look at Aria with no sales nonsense, that's what I'm here for. Email me directly.

**One last unsolicited recommendation.** If you ended up with another AI receptionist vendor and they're not working out, the most important thing to negotiate when you switch (to anyone — not just back to us) is the data-export commitment in writing. Most vendors don't volunteer it. Get it in the contract.

Wishing [Practice Name] a great rest of 2026.

[AM Name]

**CTA:** Stay in touch → [AM email]
