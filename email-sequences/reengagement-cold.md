# Re-engagement — 60+ Days Cold

**Audience:** Leads who downloaded a lead magnet, opened or replied to a few emails, then went quiet for 60+ days. Includes practices that booked a demo but no-showed and never rebooked.
**Goal:** Honest, low-pressure ask. Either get them back or clean them off the list. Voice gets progressively more direct across the four emails.
**Length:** 4 emails over 14 days.
**Voice:** Plain. Honest. Slightly self-aware. Sign as a real person.

Replace `{{first_name}}` and `{{rep_first_name}}` with your ESP's merge tags.

---

## Email 1 — Day 0: The friendly noticing email

**Subject:** Did I lose you somewhere?
**Preview:** No agenda — just checking.

Hey {{first_name}},

We chatted (or you grabbed something off our site) a couple months back, and then — radio silence. Which is completely normal. Practices get busy, priorities change, the front-desk fire of the week takes over.

I'm just checking in because I genuinely don't know where you landed.

If something specific stopped the conversation, hit reply and tell me. Pricing? Timing? Picked another vendor? Decided to hire a human? Any answer is a useful answer.

— {{rep_first_name}}
Aria Dental AI

## Email 2 — Day 4: What changed since you last looked

**Subject:** Two things that changed since you last looked
**Preview:** In case the version you saw isn't the version that exists now.

Hey {{first_name}},

Software moves fast and I'd hate for you to make a call based on an old impression. Two things that changed since we last talked:

**1. Live insurance verification ships in the call now.** Aria verifies coverage and quotes the patient's out-of-pocket while they're still on the line. It used to be a same-day-but-async step. The patient hangs up knowing what they'll pay.

**2. Setup is now 48 hours.** It used to be a week. One 30-minute discovery call from you, we configure and integrate, and you're live the next business day.

If either of those moves the needle, here's a calendar link: https://www.ariadental.ai/contact

If not, no worries — one more email next week and then I stop.

— {{rep_first_name}}

## Email 3 — Day 9: The honest one

**Subject:** Was it me?
**Preview:** I'd rather know than guess.

Hey {{first_name}},

I'm not great at pretending to not notice things, so here it is.

You looked at us. You read enough to know what we do. Then you stopped responding. The most likely explanations, in order:

1. The price was higher than what you wanted to spend right now.
2. You were polite but never actually had budget — it was research mode.
3. You signed with someone else and felt awkward telling us.
4. You decided AI isn't ready for your practice yet.
5. Life got busy.

If it's any of 1–4, I genuinely want to know. We're a small team and the only way we get sharper is hearing the real reason. One sentence is plenty.

If it's 5, no need to reply — I'll send one last note in a few days and then leave you alone.

— {{rep_first_name}}
