## Email 4 — Optimization tips (T+30 days)

**Subject:** Three tweaks most practices make in month one
**Preview text:** From 30 days of customer data, here are the optimizations that move the most numbers.
**Send timing:** T+30 days

Hi [First Name],

A pattern from our customer base: most practices that get the most out of Aria in year one make three small tweaks during month one. Here's the list, in order of impact:

**1. Tighten your appointment-type definitions.** The biggest wins come from being specific. "Adult cleaning" and "child cleaning" are different durations with different providers. The more specific your types, the better Aria's scheduling.

**2. Add a payment-required flag for new-patient consults.** If you charge a $50 deposit (refundable if they show), no-show rates drop another 30%. Aria handles the charge automatically.

**3. Turn on review-request SMS.** Aria texts a Google review link 4 hours after the visit. The practices that turn this on add 3-8 reviews a month. The practices that don't, don't.

Want me to walk you through any of these on a 15-minute call? Reply with which one.

[AM Name]

## Email 6 — Expansion conversation (T+60 days)

**Subject:** What's working and what we'd add next
**Preview text:** Quick check-in on the things customers usually want to add at the 60-day mark.
**Send timing:** T+60 days

Hi [First Name],

Two months in, here are the things customers usually want to add or change at this stage. Curious what resonates for you:

**Web chat widget.** If you haven't embedded the Aria chat widget on your website yet, this is usually the next +20% revenue capture. Same Aria, different surface — works while patients are browsing.

**Recall outreach.** If you have an old recall list (last cleaning 9+ months ago), Aria can run a one-time outreach campaign. Most practices reactivate 8-15% of the list.

**Second location.** If you have a second location and you've been waiting to evaluate Aria first — most customers expand at the 60-day mark. We can do it with no setup fee on the second site.

**SMS marketing.** We don't push this, but a few practices have asked. If your patient base is right for it, we can wire up Aria to send periodic offers (whitening promos, hygiene-month reminders, etc.) without crossing into spam territory.

Reply with which (if any) you want to talk about. Or none — that's also a fine answer.
