How It Works
Live in 48 hours.

No disruption to your practice. No training required. You do a 30-minute call — we handle configuration, integration, testing, and launch.

01

Discovery Call

30 minutes. We learn your practice — services, hours, insurance, FAQs, team, and how you talk to patients. This becomes Aria's knowledge base.

02

Configuration

We build your custom AI with your branding, colors, personality, and dental expertise. Every response is tailored to sound exactly like your practice.

03

Integration

One line of code on your website. Webhook connection to your CRM. Automated email confirmations and internal notifications to your team.

04

Testing

We test every scenario: common questions, booking flows, insurance inquiries, edge cases. We refine until Aria handles patients as well as your best team member.

05

Launch

Aria goes live. Patients start chatting, booking, and converting immediately. Your team monitors everything from the dashboard.

06

Continuous Optimization

We review conversation data weekly. Refine responses. Add capabilities. Aria gets smarter over time — continuously improving without you lifting a finger.
