Pricing FAQ

Why isn't pricing fully public?

Two reasons. First, plan fit depends on call volume and locations — we don't want practices buying the wrong tier from a number on a page. Second, we're refining 2026 pricing publicly throughout Q2. We publish ranges and we'll quote you in 24 hours; what we won't do is play games with quote-only opacity to extract maximum spend.

Annual vs monthly — what's the difference?

Default is monthly. Annual is available on Growing and standard on DSO with a negotiated rate (usually 8–15% lower). We don't push annual on Solo because most early-stage practices benefit from the flexibility to adjust as they figure out the AI receptionist workflow.

What if I need to cancel?

Monthly contracts cancel at the end of the current billing period. We don't charge cancellation fees. Annual contracts have an early-termination clause but we generally pro-rate. Read the BAA + MSA — both available on request before signing.

What counts as a "call"?

Any inbound call where Aria picks up and engages. Voicemails left without engagement don't count. Calls under 15 seconds (hang-ups, wrong numbers) don't count. Every billable call is itemized in your dashboard.

What happens if I exceed the call quota?

We don't cut you off. You get a notification at 80% and 100% of quota; if you go over, we either bill the overage at a per-call rate (disclosed in your contract) or recommend bumping to the next tier. We'd rather have you on the right plan than blowing through quotas.

Are setup fees included?

Yes for Solo and Growing — setup is included in monthly. DSO contracts sometimes include a one-time professional services fee for complex multi-PMS rollouts; this is disclosed in the quote, never a surprise.

Do you negotiate?

Modestly on Growing. More flexibly on DSO, especially for annual commitment or multi-location commitments. Solo pricing is standardized — we'd rather keep one consistent published number than play list-vs-actual games at the small end.
