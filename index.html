Common Questions
What practice owners ask us.
"We already have a receptionist."
Aria works alongside them — handling overflow, after-hours, and the repetitive tasks that burn out good staff. Or it frees them to focus entirely on case acceptance, treatment planning, and in-chair patient experience. It never calls in sick and handles unlimited calls simultaneously.
"Is it worth $7,500/month?"
A receptionist costs $70K+ per year in salary and benefits — and still misses calls, takes PTO, and handles one patient at a time. Aria handles unlimited interactions 24/7. Most practices see ROI with just 1-2 extra patients per month. The rest is pure upside.
"Will patients know it's AI?"
Aria is fully white-labeled to your practice — your name, your voice personality, your brand. The voice agent sounds natural. The chat knows your services, providers, and insurance. Patients get instant, personal help instead of hold music. The experience is often better than what a rushed receptionist delivers on a busy Monday.
"What about PMS integration?"
Aria integrates with Google Calendar, Stripe, and CRM systems via webhooks. OpenDental integration is actively being built and launches April 2026 with our first OpenDental client. Dentrix and Eaglesoft support via Sikka is next. We're transparent about what's live today and what's coming.
"Can Aria really verify insurance during a phone call?"
Yes — and no other AI receptionist can do this. When a patient calls and mentions their insurance, Aria collects the member ID and date of birth, runs a real-time eligibility check across 3,400+ payers, and reads back coverage — deductibles, remaining benefits, coinsurance — all while the patient is still on the line. This works for dependents too. A parent can call about their child, and Aria verifies the child's coverage under the parent's plan.
"Do patients have to repeat themselves across channels?"
No. Voice, chat, and SMS all share the same backend — same scheduling, same insurance verification, same payment collection. A patient who starts on chat and later calls back is recognized by phone number and greeted by name. Every channel has identical capabilities.
"How does Aria handle multiple appointments?"
If a patient says "I need a cleaning and a filling," Aria books both — checking availability for each service with the appropriate provider, then confirming everything together. Works on voice and chat. One combined confirmation covering all appointments.
"What if a parent is calling for their child?"
Aria handles dependent insurance verification natively. The parent provides policyholder information, then the child's details. Aria verifies the child's coverage under the parent's plan and reads back benefits specific to the dependent. Especially valuable for pediatric practices.
