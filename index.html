Common Questions
What practice owners ask us.
"We already have a receptionist."
Aria works alongside your team — handling overflow, after-hours, and the calls that burn out good staff. She converts hesitant callers into booked patients using empathy and live insurance verification that your receptionist can't do. Your team focuses on case acceptance and in-chair care.
"Will patients know it's AI?"
Aria is fully white-labeled to your practice. When a nervous patient says "I'm scared of the dentist," Aria responds with real empathy, not a script. The voice sounds natural, the chat knows your services and providers. Most patients say it feels better than hold music and a rushed receptionist.
"Can Aria verify insurance during the call?"
Yes — and no other AI receptionist can do this. Aria collects member ID and date of birth, verifies coverage across 3,400+ payers, reads back deductibles and remaining benefits, calculates exact out-of-pocket cost, and offers to collect — all while the patient is still on the line. Works for dependents too.
"What languages does Aria speak?"
All of them. Aria responds in whatever language the patient speaks — Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese, and 100+ more. No "press 2" menus. No hiring bilingual staff. Every non-English caller becomes a booked patient instead of a lost opportunity.
"What about no-shows?"
Aria sends automated SMS reminders at 24 hours, 2 hours, and check-in. Patients reply to confirm or reschedule. This alone cuts no-shows 30-40%. Plus digital intake forms arrive before the visit, so patients show up ready — no clipboard delays, no wasted chair time.
"Can Aria collect payment during the call?"
"I see a Visa ending in 4242 on file — would you like me to charge that?" Patient says yes, payment processed before they hang up. New patients get a Stripe link via text mid-conversation. Money in your account, zero friction.
"How do we see what Aria is doing?"
Your admin dashboard shows every appointment, conversation, lead, insurance check, and payment in real time. Weekly email reports show exactly how much revenue Aria generated. Full visibility, full control. No guessing.
"What about PMS integration?"
Aria integrates with Google Calendar, Stripe, and CRM systems today. OpenDental integration is being built right now and launches April 2026 with our first OpenDental client. Dentrix and Eaglesoft via Sikka are next.
