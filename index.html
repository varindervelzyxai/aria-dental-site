What the dashboard shows

  • Real-time count of calls Aria handled — voice, chat, and SMS.

What one-click outbound calling does

  • Pulls the recall list directly from your PMS (OpenDental live; Dentrix and Eaglesoft on roadmap).
  • Aria places outbound calls and SMS to every overdue patient in the cohort, on the schedule you set.
  • Each call is a real conversation — Aria identifies the patient, references their last visit, offers booking options.
  • Aria handles common objections (out of town, switched insurance, need to think about it) and books when the patient is ready.
  • Confirmed appointments sync straight back to your PMS calendar — no double entry.
  • You see live campaign results in the dashboard: calls placed, connections, bookings, recovered revenue.

A sample call: how Aria identifies a patient, handles “I've been busy”, and books the appointment in under a minute.

Transcript Outbound recall · 6-month overdue
Patient · 4:21 PM
Hello, this is Patient.
Aria · 4:23 PM
Hi, this is Aria from the dental clinic. I was calling because we haven't seen you in over six months and you're due for an exam and cleaning. We have openings this week. What does your availability look like?
Patient · 4:24 PM
Oh hey, thanks for calling. Yeah, I've just been so busy. I haven't had a chance to book but thanks for reaching out to me. Do you have anything available Wednesday at 2 PM?
Aria · 4:25 PM
No worries, I know life can get busy. Let me check 2 PM for you — and yes, we do have 2 PM available. I'll go ahead and put you down with Dr. Smith.
Patient · 4:25 PM
OK great, thank you so much. I'll see you Wednesday at two.
Aria · 4:25 PM
It's my pleasure. We'll see you then and you'll receive a confirmation SMS regarding your appointment as well. Take care.
What happened on the call
  • Patient identified by name from the recall list.
  • Aria explained the recall reason (6 months overdue, exam + cleaning).
  • Patient gave the “I've been busy” objection — Aria acknowledged warmly without pressure.
  • Patient suggested a time — Aria checked availability live.
  • Aria booked the appointment with a specific provider (Dr. Smith).
  • Aria mentioned the SMS confirmation that follows automatically.

Common Questions
What practice owners ask us.
"We already have a receptionist."
Aria works alongside your team — handling overflow, after-hours, and the calls that burn out good staff. She converts hesitant callers using empathy and live insurance verification your receptionist can't do. Your team focuses on case acceptance and chairside care.
"Is Aria HIPAA compliant?"
Production Voice AI is deployed under a BAA. We self-attest HIPAA Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification alignment — AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and BAAs with subprocessors where PHI may flow. Conversation storage is configurable per practice. We are not SOC 2 certified. See our full security posture →
"Will patients know it's AI?"
Aria is fully white-labeled to your practice. When a nervous patient says "I'm scared of the dentist," Aria responds with real empathy, not a script. Most patients say it feels better than hold music and a rushed receptionist. If a caller asks directly, Aria is honest — practices choose how to disclose.
"Does Aria integrate with our PMS?"
Aria integrates with calendar sync, card payments, SMS/voice, and CRM systems via webhook today. Open Dental write-back is live. Dentrix and Eaglesoft remain on the roadmap. See full integrations →
"Can Aria verify insurance during the call?"
Yes. Aria is built to verify eligibility during the live call — member ID, dependents, remaining benefits, and an out-of-pocket estimate — then offer to collect. Other vendors advertise verification; we differentiate on timing, benefit detail, and write-back where Open Dental is live. See how it works →
"What languages does Aria speak?"
Conversational support across 100+ languages, with 17 production-tuned public voices currently available across English, Spanish, Chinese and Hindi. No “press 2” menus.
"What about no-shows?"
Aria sends automated SMS reminders at 24 hours, 2 hours, and check-in. Patients reply to confirm or reschedule. This alone can reduce no-shows when patients confirm and reschedule — not a guaranteed 30–40%. Digital intake arrives before the visit, so patients show up ready — no clipboard delay.
"Can Aria collect payment during the call?"
"I see a Visa ending in 4242 on file — would you like me to charge that?" Patient says yes, payment processed before they hang up. New patients get a secure payment link via text mid-conversation. Money in your account, zero friction.
"How long does setup take?"
One 30-minute discovery call from you. We configure, integrate, and test. Calendar-only deployments can go live in about two business days after discovery. Open Dental write-back typically takes 5–10 business days. No code to write and no PMS migration. See the full process →
"What if Aria doesn't know an answer?"
Aria escalates gracefully. For clinical questions or anything outside her trained knowledge, she takes a detailed message, captures the lead, and routes it to your team — with full conversation context — so your staff calls back informed, not blind.
"What happens during outages?"
During an outage, calls failover to your existing voicemail or forward number so the line is not dead air. We do not publish an unverified uptime percentage.
"How do we see what Aria is doing?"
Your admin dashboard shows every appointment, conversation, lead, insurance check, and payment in real time. Weekly email reports show exactly how much revenue Aria generated. Full visibility, full control.
