Common Questions
What practice owners ask us.
"We already have a receptionist."
Aria works alongside your team — handling overflow, after-hours, and the calls that burn out good staff. She converts hesitant callers using empathy and live insurance verification your receptionist can't do. Your team focuses on case acceptance and chairside care.
"Is Aria HIPAA compliant?"
Yes. Aria is HIPAA compliant by design — AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, signed BAAs with every practice and every subprocessor. Conversations are stored in your private workspace. See our full security posture →
"Will patients know it's AI?"
Aria is fully white-labeled to your practice. When a nervous patient says "I'm scared of the dentist," Aria responds with real empathy, not a script. Most patients say it feels better than hold music and a rushed receptionist. If a caller asks directly, Aria is honest — practices choose how to disclose.
"Does Aria integrate with our PMS?"
Aria integrates with calendar sync, payment processing, SMS messaging, and CRM systems via webhook today. OpenDental launches April 2026 with our first OpenDental client. Dentrix and Eaglesoft (via Sikka) are next on the roadmap. See full integrations →
"Can Aria verify insurance during the call?"
Yes — and no other AI receptionist can. Aria collects member ID and date of birth, verifies across 3,400+ payers, reads back deductibles and remaining benefits, and quotes exact out-of-pocket cost while the patient is still on the line. Works for dependents too. See how it works →
"What languages does Aria speak?"
All of them. Aria responds in whatever language the patient speaks — Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese, and 100+ more. No "press 2" menus. No hiring bilingual staff. Every non-English caller becomes a booked patient instead of a lost opportunity.
"What about no-shows?"
Aria sends automated SMS reminders at 24 hours, 2 hours, and check-in. Patients reply to confirm or reschedule. This alone cuts no-shows 30-40%. Digital intake arrives before the visit, so patients show up ready — no clipboard delay.
"Can Aria collect payment during the call?"
"I see a Visa ending in 4242 on file — would you like me to charge that?" Patient says yes, payment processed before they hang up. New patients get a secure payment link via text mid-conversation. Money in your account, zero friction.
"How long does setup take?"
One 30-minute discovery call from you. We configure, integrate, and test. Live in 48 hours. No code to write, no PMS migration, no training your staff. See the full process →
"What if Aria doesn't know an answer?"
Aria escalates gracefully. For clinical questions or anything outside her trained knowledge, she takes a detailed message, captures the lead, and routes it to your team — with full conversation context — so your staff calls back informed, not blind.
"What happens during outages?"
Calls failover to your existing voicemail or forward number, so you never lose a patient to dead air. Status page is public. Practices on Aria today see 99.95%+ uptime; our infra runs on the same cloud your bank does.
"How do we see what Aria is doing?"
Your admin dashboard shows every appointment, conversation, lead, insurance check, and payment in real time. Weekly email reports show exactly how much revenue Aria generated. Full visibility, full control.
