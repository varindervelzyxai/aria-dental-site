Reviews + testimonials feed three SEO mechanisms:
1. Google Business Profile star rating (drives local-pack inclusion).
2. G2/Capterra star rating (drives directory ranking).
3. Schema.org `AggregateRating` + `Review` markup (rich snippets in SERP).

**Targets for Batch 1:** WizKids Dental, Newport Institute for Dentistry, plus any other early customer who'll go on record.

**Workflow:**

1. **Pick 3–5 customer contacts** from your CRM. Pick people who answered the phone happily last time you called.
2. **Send the testimonial request email** (template below). Offer a $50 credit to their next month or a coffee gift card to make it frictionless.
3. **Format options to offer the customer (let them pick):**
   - 2-sentence quote (easiest — most will pick this)
   - 60-second voice memo via WhatsApp (best for video reuse)
   - Short video selfie via Loom (highest impact, lowest pickup)
4. **Where to display each testimonial:**
   - Quote → homepage hero or dedicated `<section>` on `/portfolio`
   - Voice memo → embed as `<audio>` on `/demos` page with permission
   - Video → embed on homepage + dedicated case study page

   - `Review` schema per testimonial (author, reviewBody, reviewRating)
   - `AggregateRating` on the Organization JSON-LD once you have ≥3 reviews
6. **Get the customer to also post the same review on:**
   - Google Business Profile (most important — drives local pack)
